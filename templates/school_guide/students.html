A student account is never self-registered — staff always create it, one at a time or many at once — and it always starts on a one-time password the student must replace at first sign-in.

The students list

Students is the full list of everyone currently enrolled, searchable by name or admission number and filterable by class. Clicking a name opens that one student's own page: their details, their class history, their results so far, and buttons to edit them, print their account slip, or reset their password.

Registering one student

Register Student creates the student record, enrols them in the class and session you choose, and generates their admission number and login in one step. The admission number is always generated from your school's own numbering rules — nobody types one in by hand, so it can never clash with another student's.

Bringing in existing students at once

If your school already has students in a spreadsheet, use Bulk import (CSV) from the Students page instead of typing each one in. Download the template, fill in a row per student (name, gender, class, and optionally a guardian's name, email and phone), and upload it. Each row that is valid becomes exactly what registering one student by hand would create; a row that is not — a missing name, an unrecognised gender, a class that does not exist, a bad email — is skipped and reported by line number, and every other row in the file is still imported.

The generated usernames and one-time passwords for an import are shown once, on the results page, and can be downloaded from there as a CSV. They are never stored anywhere in readable form afterwards, so copy or download them before you navigate away.

A third way in: the entrance exam

A student can also arrive through the {{ page('admissions', 'admissions waitlist') }}, in the Entrance workspace: once a candidate has sat their entrance exam, an admissions officer can admit them straight into a real student record, the same way as the other two paths above.

A student's account afterwards

From a student's own page you can edit their details, deactivate them (which also switches their login off), reset their password to a new one-time password, or switch their login on or off without touching anything else about their record. Enrolment history — previous schools, earlier levels — is kept separately and can be corrected with a reason if it was entered wrong.
