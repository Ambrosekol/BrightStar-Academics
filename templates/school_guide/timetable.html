Build the exam or test timetable as a draft, check it, then release it all at once.

Building the draft

Under Exam timetable, choose the term with the tabs at the top and the session beside it, then + Add paper: class, subject, test or examination, date, start and end time, and an optional venue. With Keep this open for the next paper ticked, the form opens again after each save with the same class, day, room and type, so a class's whole week goes in quickly. Papers can be edited (Edit opens the same form) or deleted freely while they are a draft — nothing outside staff sees them yet.

The papers show Day by day, each day with its papers in time order, or as a Grid of days against classes. A paper that overlaps another for the same class, or uses a room another paper is using at the same time, is marked as a clash and says what it overlaps; the count of clashes sits at the top. A solid dot means released, a dashed one draft.

Releasing

Releasing turns every not-yet-released entry for the session and term into a released one, in one action, and — in the background, so you are not left waiting — notifies every currently enrolled student of the classes it covers, and their parents: an in-app alert each, plus an email to the guardian contact. Releasing again releases nothing more, and tells you so.

Releasing is its own permission, separate from adding or editing entries, so a school can decide exactly who is trusted to notify everyone at once.

What students and parents see

A student sees only the released entries for their own class; a linked parent sees the same for their child. Either can download it as a PDF or print it directly from the page.
