One platform.
Every school on its own portal.

Brightstars Academics runs entrance examinations, school administration, results and finance. Each school gets a secure portal of its own — its own address, its own database, its own files — with nothing shared between schools.

Platform console

Entrance examinations

Question banks, candidate registration, timed computer-based papers with server-side scoring, rankings and exportable results.

School administration

Sessions, classes, subjects, enrolment and promotion, assignments and assessments, and term results with a verify, approve and release workflow.

Students and parents

Students sit assignments and assessments and track released results. Parents follow their children's results, attendance and fees.

Finance and library

Fee items, payments and allocations, outstanding balances, receipts by PDF, email or WhatsApp, and a book and loan catalogue.

Separate by design

Every school has its own database and its own folder of files. One school can never read, reach or affect another's data.

Your own address

A school's portal answers on the school's own domain, so to staff and parents it is the school's system, not someone else's.

How a school joins

  1. We create the school on the platform with its name, branding and logo.
  2. It gets a working portal address straight away, at yourschool.{{ portal_domain }}.
  3. To use its own address, the school adds a CNAME record on its domain pointing at that portal address.
  4. Its administrator signs in and sets up staff, classes and everything else.
