The Entrance workspace runs the exam a candidate sits before they are ever a student here — kept deliberately apart from the School workspace (see Welcome).

Question banks and exam configuration

Question Banks hold the questions themselves; Exam Configuration decides which bank is the live paper for each entry class and subject, for the current academic period. A bank being used by a live exam can be locked to stop it changing mid-way. Opening a bank has an Export JSON button, so its exact questions and answer key can be kept outside the portal, or copied into another school. Each question also has an up and down arrow, so the order candidates see them in can be changed any time after it is written, not only fixed at the moment it was added.

Candidates

Candidates registers who is sitting the exam and prints or resets their sign-in credentials. A candidate signs in with those credentials, sits the paper once, and their attempt is recorded automatically as they go.

A candidate's own record has a Delete Candidate button, for School Admin only. It permanently removes the person and every attempt and answer they ever made — there is no undo, and it is meant for a genuine mistake (a duplicate registration, say), not for someone who simply did not get in; declining them from the admissions waitlist keeps their record intact instead.

Results and rankings

Results & Analytics shows every candidate's outcome, one subject at a time, so you can compare how everyone did in Mathematics, or in English, without the other subjects in the way. Rankings instead orders every candidate by their overall score, highest first — the list a school actually uses to decide who is offered admission; it can export what it shows as a CSV spreadsheet, and export the underlying raw attempts as CSV or JSON, for further work outside the portal. A candidate's own record has a Share Result Image button once they have completed all three papers: a single branded picture of their cumulative result, carrying the school's own logo and colours and nothing of another school's, ready to send however is easiest.

Examination Activity

Examination Activity is a plain, newest-first list of every paper ever started, by anyone, for any subject — not analysed or ranked, just a record: who started which paper, when, and what became of it. It is the page to check when something seems to have gone wrong with one particular sitting, rather than with a whole subject's results.

Entrance practice

Practice Tests lets anyone, with no registration, try a practice paper for the class they are aiming for — from either the last session's own entrance exam, or a custom bank the school sets up for practice. The current session's own live bank is never offered for practice, so it cannot reveal an exam that has not been sat yet.

From candidate to student

Once a candidate has sat at least one paper, they move onto the {{ page('admissions', 'admissions waitlist') }} — where an admissions officer decides to admit them into a real, enrolled student account, or decline them.
