Once a candidate has sat at least one paper, they are on the admissions waitlist — the bridge between the Entrance workspace and the School workspace.

The waitlist

Admissions lists every candidate who has attempted at least one paper, ranked by their overall percentage, filterable by entry level and by whether a decision has been made yet. A candidate who has never sat a paper does not appear at all.

Admitting

Admitting creates exactly what Register Student creates by hand: the student record, an enrolment in the class you choose for the current session, and a login with a generated admission number and a one-time password. The candidate's name is split onto the form as a starting guess — correct it if it split wrongly before confirming. If the candidate has a guardian email or phone on file, leaving the box ticked also creates a parent portal account from those same details, so the whole family reaches the portal in one action.

Admitting is final. An admitted candidate cannot be admitted again, declined, or put back on the waitlist — by then a real student and a real login exist. Double-check the class and the corrected name before confirming.

Declining

Declining takes a candidate off the waitlist, with an optional reason, and — unlike admitting — can be undone at any time, putting them straight back to pending.

Who can do this

Seeing the waitlist at all, not only deciding it, needs Admit candidates — a separate permission from simply viewing candidates, so a school decides exactly who is trusted to make admission decisions. The Admissions Officer preset carries it; nobody else does by default. An officer limited to one entry level's candidates (see Roles, permissions and scope) can only decide for that level.
