A mark goes through the same journey everywhere it comes from — a test, an assignment, a manually entered score — before it ever reaches a student or parent.

Entered, verified, approved, released

Under Results & Records, a result moves through four stages: entered, verified, approved, released. Only released results count toward a student's official term result and their report card; the earlier stages let more than one person check a mark before it becomes visible to anyone outside staff. A school can release a whole class's results in one action, with a confirmation, instead of one subject at a time.

How a term result is worked out

Each subject's term result is Continuous Assessment (test, assignment and project, out of 40 between them, in shares your school sets once) plus an exam out of 60. Work that has been removed stops counting; the arithmetic is worked out fresh every time it is needed, from the results themselves, so it can never disagree with what is actually on record.

Report cards

A student's report card for a term appears — to the student, a linked parent, and staff — the moment every one of that student's results for the term has been released, whether by hand or by a session's own release date. One result still waiting holds the whole card back. It shows the school's own branding, each subject's marks and grade, the overall percentage, the class average, and (once at least one has been rated) affective and psychomotor trait ratings, alongside the class teacher's comment and the head's signature.

A class teacher writes the comment and rates traits — Punctuality, Neatness, Leadership and the like — from the same page, and can do so before results are even released. Whoever last changes a comment or a rating is credited for it; saving the page unchanged never takes over someone else's.

When a card becomes ready, the parent is told automatically — an in-app alert, an email and a WhatsApp message — that it is ready to view and download. Nobody has to remember to announce it.
