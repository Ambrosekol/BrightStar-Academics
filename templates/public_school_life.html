SCHOOL LIFE

Learning continues beyond the classroom.

At Creative Rainbow Montessori School, children are encouraged to participate, explore, create, compete, collaborate and grow. School life gives every child opportunities to discover interests, develop confidence and build lasting character.

BEYOND ACADEMICS

A school experience built around participation.

Children learn in many different ways. Alongside classroom learning, Creative Rainbow provides opportunities for pupils and students to develop through sports, social activities, games, practical experiences, creative participation and other forms of enrichment.

These experiences help children discover their strengths, work with others, take responsibility and become more confident participants in their school community.

Sports & Physical Development

Sport encourages discipline, teamwork, healthy competition and the confidence to keep improving.

Football Academy

Football provides an avenue for children to develop teamwork, commitment, physical ability and sporting confidence.

Social & Cultural Activities

School activities create opportunities for children to participate, express themselves and celebrate community together.

Games & Recreation

Play, games and recreation contribute to a balanced school experience while helping children build friendships and confidence.
