Everything in the School workspace — students, subjects, assignments, results, attendance, timetables — is organised around classes, sessions and terms.

Classes

Every school starts with the standard list already in place — Primary 1 to 6, JSS 1 to 3, SSS 1 to 3 — under Classes, so this is usually the one step already done on the setup checklist. You can rename, reorder or add to this list if your school uses different names.

Sessions and terms

An academic session is a school year, e.g. "2025/2026" — set up and switched under Academic Sessions. One session is always the current one; new enrolments, results and attendance are filed against it. A term is First, Second or Third Term (or "Full Session" for an annual view) — terms are just a choice on a form, not something you create.

Subjects

Subjects are not seeded: a brand-new school has none, which is the second step of the setup checklist. Creating a subject attaches it to one or more classes at the same time, so it only ever shows up where it is actually taught. A subject can be locked once its results are being finalised, which stops further changes to it for that class.

Moving a class up a session

At the end of a session, Promotion generates a draft of who moves where — promote, repeat, transfer, graduate, hold or withdraw — one student at a time or as a batch, using progression rules you set once (e.g. "JSS 1 → JSS 2"). A draft must be approved before it can be committed, and committing is what actually moves everyone's enrolment into the new session.
