ABOUT CREATIVE RAINBOW

Education with purpose.
Growth with character.

Creative Rainbow Montessori School is a learning community in Amuwo-Odofin, Lagos State, where children are encouraged to learn, participate, develop confidence and grow as capable, responsible and well-rounded individuals.

01WHO WE ARE

OUR STORY

A school built around the whole child.

At Creative Rainbow, we see education as more than the completion of lessons. Children need knowledge, guidance, character, opportunities to participate and an environment where they can discover their strengths.

Our approach brings these dimensions together so that academic development and personal growth can take place side by side.

✦

OUR APPROACH

Growing children with purpose.

At Creative Rainbow, we believe that children thrive when they are given the right environment to learn, develop confidence, build character and discover their strengths.

Our approach brings together academic development, positive values, responsibility, participation and the guidance children need as they grow.

LEADERSHIP

Guided by a clear purpose.

Mrs. Atuchukwu Chioma, Proprietress of Creative Rainbow Montessori School, provides leadership grounded in the belief that the development of children includes both education and the values that shape how they live and relate with others.

This vision is reflected in the school's emphasis on learning, humility, responsibility, good conduct, confidence and the development of each child.
