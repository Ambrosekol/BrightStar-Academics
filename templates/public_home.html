MORE THAN A SCHOOL

A place where education meets character.

Creative Rainbow Montessori School is a learning community in Amuwo-Odofin, Lagos State, committed to the academic, personal and character development of children.

Our school community is guided by a simple conviction: education should help children become capable learners, responsible people and confident participants in the world around them.

OUR APPROACH

Helping every child grow with purpose.

At Creative Rainbow, we believe children thrive when they are given the right environment to learn, develop confidence, build character and discover their strengths.

Our school community brings together academic development, positive values, responsibility, participation and the guidance children need as they grow.
