The School portal's home page (Overview) is made for the person looking at it. Nobody sees a general page: each part only appears when your role and your teaching duties call for it, so a bursar sees money and never a ranking of students, and a subject teacher sees their own subjects' marks and nothing about fees. Your name and your level (for example Head teacher or Class teacher) are at the top.

What each person sees

{{ shot('overview', 'The Overview as the School Admin sees it: the setup checklist strip, then the whole school\'s figures. Someone with another role sees only their own part.') }}
  • Proprietor / School Admin: the setup checklist, the whole school this term (students, classes, subjects, work, and how many marks are ready to release or still with teachers, class by class, and any class with no class teacher yet), and the top student of every class.
  • Head teacher: the same whole-school picture and top students, without the setup checklist.
  • Class teacher: a card for each class they are in charge of: whether today's register has been taken, marks ready for them to release, marks the subject teachers have not sent yet, how many report card comments are written, and the class's top student.
  • Subject teacher: each subject they teach in each class: how many students are marked, the marks still to send, the class average against last term and against the school, the spread of scores, the grades across all their students, who needs support, and work waiting to be graded.
  • Bursar and finance officers: collections today, month to date and this session (each against yesterday or last month), the last 30 days and the last six months as charts, how payments were made, fees owed, the collection rate overall, by class and by fee item, and the largest balances. A cashier who only records payments sees only their own takings.
  • Librarian: titles, copies on the shelf, books on loan and overdue loans.
  • Admissions officer: candidates waiting for an admissions decision.
  • Whoever answers parents: new conversations nobody has taken yet, open and in progress.
  • Secretary and records officers: students registered this month, SSS students with no department, and students with no parent account.

Reading the numbers and charts

  • Every headline number has a comparison beneath it: the term before, last session, yesterday, the same days last month or the week before. ▲ and ▼ show the direction; green means the change is good, red that it is not (fewer new parent complaints is good, so that one turns green going down).
  • Bars are sorted largest first. A thin grey tick on a bar marks the comparison: last term for class and subject averages, the school average on a teacher's own class, last session for enrolment.
  • The results bar runs light to dark: not yet sent by the subject teacher, ready to release, released.
  • Spread (for a subject teacher) shows the lowest and highest student in a class, with the class average as a dot.
  • A yellow tile is something waiting on someone: marks to release, a register not taken, fees owed, books overdue.
  • Hover over (or tab to) any bar, column or point to see its exact figure, and open Table under a chart to read every value as a list.

Someone with several roles sees every part their roles allow. At the bottom, Go to lists only the pages your role can open. The academic parts count one term at a time: the latest term with results, or the one you choose at the top right.

Finish setting up your school

Only the School Admin sees this checklist. A new school has four steps to finish before it can really run: review its classes (the standard list is already there, so usually this is done), add its subjects, enrol its students, and set up its fee items. Each step shows as done when the school has the data for it, and it links straight to the page where that happens. See {{ page('classes-subjects', 'Classes and subjects') }}, {{ page('students', 'Students') }} and {{ page('finance', 'Fees, payments and receipts') }}.

Closing the checklist (Hide this checklist) folds it into a small bar at the top of the Overview that still shows how far setup has got; Show setup checklist brings it back, open. Both happen in place: the button spins for a moment and only the checklist changes, so the rest of the Overview stays where it is. Closing it hides it for the whole school, as it always has. Its progress is refreshed about every fifteen minutes, so a step you have just finished may take that long to show as done.

Top of the Class

This card shows the highest-scoring student in each class for the chosen term and session (for a class teacher, only their own classes; staff outside the teaching chain never see it). A class appears only once its report card results for that term are released, so a class with no released results is left off rather than shown empty. The leader is featured at the top, and the other classes follow with their percentage and grade. The term and session are shown in the card's heading.

The score is the same one on the student's report card, so the two always agree. Changing a result changes the card once it is recalculated.

The panel at the bottom right

The panel holds the setup checklist and the What's new notices. It opens minimised, so it does not cover the page when you arrive. Read more about it in {{ page('staff-admin', 'Staff, roles and branding') }}, under The panel at the bottom right.

Where to go from here

The Go to links at the bottom, and the buttons on each card, open the pages behind them. If something you expect is missing, the reason is almost always your role or your teaching duties. See {{ page('roles-permissions', 'Roles, permissions and scope') }}.
