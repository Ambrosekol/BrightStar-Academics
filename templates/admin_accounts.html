How staff access works

Each member of staff holds one or more roles, and can do everything any of them allows. Teaching duties (the class they are class teacher of, the subjects they teach) keep a teacher to their own classes. Other limits narrow that further, to chosen sessions, classes, subjects or question banks. The School Admin has full access. Suspending someone stops them signing in at once; their record is kept and they can be reactivated at any time.
