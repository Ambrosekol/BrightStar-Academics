Finance covers what a school charges, what it has received, and the paper (or PDF) that proves it. Routes: {{ file('blueprints/finance/routes.py') }}. Rules, balance arithmetic and receipts: {{ file('blueprints/finance/helpers.py') }}. Tables: {{ file('models/finance.py') }}.

The four things

ThingTableMeaning
Fee item{{ table('finance_fee_items') }}, {{ table('finance_fee_item_classes') }}A line in the school's fee catalogue: "Tuition (JSS 1)", amount, category, applicability (Full Session, a term, or One-time), required or optional, and which classes it applies to.
Fee assessment{{ table('finance_fee_assessments') }}A fee charged to one student for one session (and term): "this child owes this much for this". Created from fee items on the assess form.
Payment{{ table('finance_payments') }}Money received: amount, category, method, reference, who paid, the date, who recorded it, an optional note, and a status (posted or voided).
Allocation{{ table('finance_payment_allocations') }}How much of a payment is applied to which assessment. Recording a payment does not apply it: allocating is a separate step.

What "paid" means

"Paid" for a fee always means allocated to that specific fee. A fee is Paid, Part Paid or Unpaid computed to the kobo (_payment_status) so a floating-point crumb never flips a status. A payment that has been recorded but not yet allocated appears as an unallocated credit in the student's totals, so a just-made payment is never invisible, and never silently hides debt on an unrelated fee. Balances are summed across every session, so a debt left from last year is not hidden when the school moves on. See _finance_student_outstanding and _finance_student_lifetime_totals.

Money is entered as naira and kept to the kobo. _money refuses NaN, infinity and absurd sizes; the amount in words on a receipt stops at the billions.

Receipt numbers

Each payment gets a number <PREFIX>-<year>-<5 digits> ({{ file('blueprints/finance/helpers.py') }}, _next_receipt_no). The prefix is a per-school setting (defaulting to the school's code) so a school keeps numbering the same way for ever. Numbers are unique per school (a database constraint), never shared or borrowed across schools.

Voiding

A wrong payment is voided with a reason, never deleted. Its receipt stays in the audit history, stays viewable, is marked VOIDED in big letters on the page, the printout, the PDF, and the parent's copy, is never emailed or sent by WhatsApp, and its allocations stop counting. Permission: finance.manage.

The receipt: one design per school

A receipt is drawn in the school's own identity: its two brand colours, logo (or its initials if it has none), name, motto, address, phone and email, its authorised signature image, the name of the member of staff who recorded the payment, the note the bursar attached, the student's name, admission number and class, the session, the method and reference, the amount in figures and in words, and a small account summary (fees charged, paid to date and balance due as at that payment, so re-printing an old receipt never shows a balance later payments have changed).

A school with no colours of its own gets the portal defaults. Nothing about the school is hard-coded: an earlier version drew one fixed blue-wave design for every school, which is exactly what this replaced.

To change the look, edit static/receipt.css and core/receipt_pdf.py together; a school's signature is set on Receipt settings ({{ route('admin_finance_receipt_settings') }}), drawn on a pad or uploaded, stored under uploads/signatures/.

Telling parents

Recording a payment ({{ route('admin_finance_record') }}) automatically:

  1. adds an in-app alert for the child's linked parent accounts (_notify_parents_payment_recorded in {{ file('core/notifications.py') }});
  2. emails the guardian the receipt as a PDF with amount, date, purpose, balance and the bursar's note in the body; and
  3. sends the receipt PDF by WhatsApp.

Steps 2 and 3 run as the durable 'send_payment_receipt' job ({{ file('core/jobs.py') }}) via _send_payment_receipt_to_guardian, and every attempt is written to {{ table('finance_delivery_logs') }}, so the bursar can see on the receipt page whether it arrived and why not. Sending by hand (Send by email, Send by WhatsApp) uses the same senders and logs the same way; who may send is finance.receipt.send.

Contact details come from the student's record (guardian_email, guardian_phone). Phone numbers are converted to international form (_ng_phone). The WhatsApp Cloud API only delivers free-form messages within 24 hours of the recipient's last message to the school's number; reaching parents outside that window needs approved message templates, which this application does not send.

Online payments (Paystack)

A school connects its own Paystack account under Online Payments ({{ route('admin_finance_paystack_settings') }}); there is no shared platform account, so a school with nothing saved simply has no "Pay online" option. Everything lives in {{ file('core/payments.py') }} and {{ file('blueprints/finance/paystack.py') }}; the secret key is encrypted at rest the same way {{ file('core/delivery.py') }} encrypts a mail password, under its own key-derivation domain (a compromise of one says nothing about the other), in {{ table('school_payment_settings') }}.

A parent starting a payment from {{ route('parent_child_finance') }} writes a {{ table('finance_online_payments') }} row ('pending', with a reference this application generates) before Paystack is ever asked to open a transaction, then is redirected to whatever address Paystack's own transaction/initialize gives back. Two independent things can report what happened next: Paystack redirecting the browser to {{ route('parent_finance_pay_callback') }}, and Paystack calling {{ route('paystack_webhook') }} directly, server to server (checked against its HMAC-SHA512 signature, computed with the school's own secret key, before anything in its body is read as fact). Both paths call the same _finalize, which asks Paystack's own transaction/verify/<reference> what really happened — never trusting the callback's query string or the webhook's body on their own — and a conditional UPDATE ... WHERE status = 'pending' is the actual claim that lets only the first of the two arrivals act on one reference.

A confirmed online payment becomes an ordinary {{ table('finance_payments') }} row, through the same fields a manually recorded payment gets (method='Paystack', the reference, Paystack's own confirmed amount — never what the parent's request claimed), and reuses the exact same 'send_payment_receipt' job. FinancePayment.recorded_by is not nullable — no member of staff acted on this particular payment, so it is attributed to whoever last configured the school's own Paystack settings, which is an honest answer to "who is responsible for this channel existing" rather than a claim that they typed it in.

Starting a payment is @idempotent_write('parent.pay_online') ({{ file('core/idempotency.py') }}): a resubmitted click is sent to the same Paystack checkout address again rather than opening a second transaction for the same click.

Permissions

  • {{ perm('finance.view_all') }} sees every payment; {{ perm('finance.view_own') }} sees only payments the person recorded (a cashier).
  • {{ perm('finance.record') }} records payments. {{ perm('finance.manage') }} manages fee items, voids payments and sets the receipt signature.
  • {{ perm('finance.receipt.send') }} sends a receipt by email or WhatsApp.
  • {{ perm('finance.paystack.manage') }} sets up, tests and removes the school's own Paystack account.
