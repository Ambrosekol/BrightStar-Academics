Finance has four things, and most confusion comes from mixing them up ({{ file('blueprints/finance/routes.py') }}, {{ file('blueprints/finance/helpers.py') }}):

ThingTableMeaning
Fee item{{ table('finance_fee_items') }}A line in the fee list ("Tuition, JSS 1, ₦85,000"), for chosen classes.
Bill (assessment){{ table('finance_fee_assessments') }}A fee charged to one student for a session: "this child owes this".
Payment{{ table('finance_payments') }}Money received, with its receipt.
Allocation{{ table('finance_payment_allocations') }}How much of a payment pays which bill. A fee is only "paid" once a payment is allocated to it.

Balances and allocation

A parent paid, but the child still shows as owing

You see
The payment is on record, but the fee account, the parent's portal or New students to bill still shows money owed.
Why

Recording a payment does not by itself say which fee it pays. Until it is allocated, it is "received, not yet applied". Every admin page carries a banner while any payment is unallocated: "… has been collected but not yet allocated to a fee item. Allocate now →".

Fix

Open the payment (Finance › Payments, or the banner's link to {{ route('admin_finance_unallocated') }}) and choose Apply to fees. Allocate it across the student's bills.

Check
The fee shows Paid; the banner disappears once nothing is left to apply.

Allocation is refused

You see
"Allocation for … exceeds its outstanding balance." · "The total allocation exceeds the payment balance." · "… has already been fully paid and cannot receive a further allocation." · "A payment can only be allocated to assessments belonging to the same student." · "Only a posted payment can be allocated."
Why

An allocation may not pay more than a bill still owes, more than the payment has left, a bill of another student, or come from a voided payment.

Fix

Lower the amounts so they fit. Money left over after every bill is paid stays as unapplied credit on the student; if it was paid for the wrong child, void it (with a reason) and record it against the right one.

Check
The allocation saves.

Billing a student is refused

You see
"The selected student has no active class enrolment for the selected academic session." · "Select at least one fee item…" · "One or more selected fee items are no longer available."
Why

A bill is for a student in a class in a session, and a fee item applies to particular classes. A student with no class this session, or a fee not set for their class, cannot be billed.

Fix

Enrol the student in a class for the current session (their record › Edit). Make sure the fee item lists that class (Fee structure › the item › classes).

Check
Billing saves, and the student moves from Not billed yet to Waiting for payment on {{ route('admin_finance_new_students') }}.

A cashier cannot open a student's fee account

You see
"This action is not available to you" when a Finance Records Officer opens a student's account.
Why

By design. A whole account shows every payment, whoever recorded it, so it needs {{ perm('finance.view_all') }}. A cashier ({{ perm('finance.view_own') }}) sees and works with their own payments.

Fix

If the person needs to see every account, give them a role with View all finance (the Finance Manager role has it).

Check
They can open accounts after their role changes (within BRIGHTSTARS_ADMIN_CACHE_SECONDS).

A student is missing from, or stuck on, "New students to bill"

You see
A new student is not on the list; or a student who paid is still listed.
Why

The list ({{ file('blueprints/finance/new_students.py') }}, {{ table('finance_new_students') }}) holds students created since the list existed (admitted, registered or imported), not older students or ones created already archived. A student leaves only when everything billed is allocated in full.

Fix

Still listed after paying: allocate the payment (above). Not wanted on the list (a scholarship): a Finance Manager ticks them, gives a reason, and takes them off; they can be put back from Done.

Check
The student moves to Done.

Online payments (Paystack)

A school uses its own Paystack account (there is no shared platform account), set by the School Admin under Settings › Online payments ({{ route('admin_finance_paystack_settings') }}). A payment is confirmed only by asking Paystack what happened, never by trusting the browser ({{ file('blueprints/finance/paystack.py') }}, {{ file('core/payments.py') }}).

Parents see no "Pay online" button

You see
The parent's fee page has no pay button; or "This school has not set up online payments yet."
Why

The school has no keys saved.

Fix

The School Admin saves the public key (pk_live_…) and secret key (sk_live_…) from the Paystack dashboard, presses Test, and pastes the webhook URL shown on that page into Paystack (Settings › API Keys & Webhooks).

Check
Test says "Connected to Paystack (live mode)."

The connection test fails

You see
"Paystack did not accept this secret key." · "Connected, but your public key and secret key are from different modes (one test, one live). Use a matching pair." · "Paystack could not be reached: …"
Why

A wrong or revoked secret key; a test key paired with a live one; or the server cannot reach api.paystack.co.

Fix

Copy both keys again from the same mode in the Paystack dashboard. If it cannot be reached, check the server's outbound internet (curl -sI https://api.paystack.co).

Check
The test succeeds.

"This payment is still being confirmed." / a payment stays pending

You see
The parent paid, but the payment is not on the child's account yet.
Why

Two things report a payment back: the parent's browser returning, and Paystack's webhook. A parent who paid by transfer or USSD may never return, and a webhook fails if its URL was not set or changed. Any payment still pending after two minutes is checked with Paystack directly whenever the finance overview ({{ route('admin_finance_dashboard') }}), a parent's fee page or the store opens.

Fix
  1. Open Finance's Collections page: that checks pending payments with Paystack.
  2. Make sure the webhook URL in the Paystack dashboard is exactly the one shown on Online payments ({{ route('paystack_webhook') }}), on the school's own address, over https.
  3. If Paystack says it failed, the parent sees "This payment was not successful…" and pays again.
Check
The payment appears, with its receipt sent to the guardian.

Both the browser and the webhook can arrive at once: a conditional update ensures only the first records the payment, so a payment is never recorded twice.

A refund is refused or stays pending

You see
"A reason is required to refund a payment." · "Only a posted payment can be refunded." · "A refund for this payment already exists (status: …)." · "Online payments are not set up for this school any more, so Paystack cannot be asked for a refund." · a refund shown as pending.
Why

A refund asks Paystack to send money back for a payment made online. Pending refunds are checked with Paystack when the finance overview opens. Voiding is different: it cancels a payment's record (with a reason) and moves no money.

Fix

Give a reason; refund only posted, online payments; wait for Paystack (refunds can take days with the bank). For cash paid back in person, void the payment instead.

Check
The refund shows processed.

The store

"The store is not open yet" / parents cannot buy online

You see
"The store is not open yet: the school has no current session." — or — "The school takes payment for the store at the office. You can see what is available here."
Why

Purchases belong to a session, so a current session is needed. Buying online needs the school's Paystack account.

Fix

Set a current session (Settings › Academic sessions). Set up Online payments for online buying.

Check
The parents' Store shows items with a buy button.

A purchase is marked "short"

You see
Purchase claims shows a purchase as short.
Why

Stock is taken with a check that can never sell the last one twice. An online payment that completed after the last one was sold is still recorded (the money is in) and marked short for the office to settle ({{ file('blueprints/store/routes.py') }}).

Fix

Restock the item and hand it over, or cancel the purchase with a reason and refund the parent.

Check
The purchase is collected or cancelled.
