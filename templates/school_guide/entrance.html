The Entrance workspace runs the exam a candidate sits before they are ever a student here — kept deliberately apart from the School workspace (see Welcome).

Question banks and exam configuration

Question Banks hold the questions themselves; Exam Configuration decides which bank is the live paper for each entry class and subject, for the current academic period. A bank being used by a live exam can be locked to stop it changing mid-way. Opening a bank has an Export JSON button, so its exact questions and answer key can be kept outside the portal, or copied into another school. Each question also has an up and down arrow, so the order candidates see them in can be changed any time after it is written, not only fixed at the moment it was added.

A question can be tagged with a senior Department (Science, Art or Commercial) on its form, or with "department" in an imported bank file. When an SSS paper's bank has tagged questions, every candidate gets an equal share from each of the three departments, and their answers there suggest the department to admit them into (see {{ page('admissions', 'admissions') }}). The standard SSS 1 General Knowledge bank comes with Science, Art and Commercial questions already tagged; a school whose banks were installed before this can tag its own.

Candidates

Candidates registers who is sitting the exam and prints or resets their sign-in credentials. A candidate signs in with those credentials, sits the paper once, and their attempt is recorded automatically as they go.

A candidate's own record has a Delete candidate button (under Print and share), for School Admin only. It permanently removes the person and every attempt and answer they ever made — there is no undo, and it is meant for a genuine mistake (a duplicate registration, say), not for someone who simply did not get in; declining them from the admissions waitlist keeps their record intact instead.

Sitting the entrance examination

A candidate signs in on the school's sign-in page with the code and password printed for them on Candidates. Their dashboard lists their papers (one for each subject of their entry class) as Not started, In progress or Completed.

  • One paper at a time. Start begins that paper's timer, kept by the server. The paper looks like a school test in exam mode: one question at a time, a question map, the time left, and full screen.
  • Answers are saved as they go. If the power or the connection goes, the candidate signs in again (here or on another computer) and continues with their answers. The clock keeps running.
  • When the time is up, the paper is submitted with the answers given.
  • Scores are not shown to candidates. The school sees them on Results; a candidate learns the outcome when the school releases it with their admission.
  • A second attempt (a power cut ruined the first) is given on the candidate's page with Grant retake, once the first attempt is finished.

Results and rankings

Results has four tabs, each filtered by entry level and searchable by name or candidate code. By subject shows every candidate's outcome one subject at a time, side by side, so you can compare how everyone did in Mathematics, or in English, without the other subjects in the way; Summary is one row per candidate with all three papers and the total, and prints; Examination activity is described below. Rankings instead orders every candidate by their overall score, highest first — the list a school actually uses to decide who is offered admission; it can export what it shows as a CSV spreadsheet, and By subject exports the underlying raw attempts as CSV or JSON, for further work outside the portal. A candidate's own record has a Result image button once they have completed all three papers: a single branded picture of their cumulative result, carrying the school's own logo and colours and nothing of another school's, ready to send however is easiest.

Examination Activity

Examination Activity is a plain, newest-first list of every paper ever started, by anyone, for any subject — not analysed or ranked, just a record: who started which paper, when, and what became of it. It is the page to check when something seems to have gone wrong with one particular sitting, rather than with a whole subject's results.

Entrance practice

Anyone, with no registration and no sign-in, can practise for the entrance examination on the school's public practice page (its address is on Practice Tests, ready to share with parents and on the school's website). They choose the class they are applying for, then a subject.

Where practice questions come from

On Practice Tests, each class and subject has its source:

  • Question pool (recommended, and the starting choice): every earlier session's entrance paper for that class and subject, a practice bank if you choose one, and the platform's ready-made practice questions, each question once. Every attempt draws its questions at random from the whole pool, so the bigger the pool, the less a retake repeats. The page shows each subject's pool ("25 questions an attempt, from a pool of 480").
  • Last session's examination only: just the most recent earlier paper.
  • Practice bank only: a bank the school writes for practice (in Question Banks).
  • Per attempt: how many questions one attempt draws (blank: the examination's own number). The time allowed keeps the examination's time for each question.

Ready-made practice questions

Every school has, from its first day, between 200 and 475 ready-made practice questions for each class and subject:

  • Mathematics: arithmetic, fractions, percentages, money, measures and angles for Year 7 / JSS 1; algebra, indices, standard form, interest, geometry, statistics, sets, number bases and probability for Year 10 / SSS 1.
  • English: meanings and opposites, verb forms, plurals, concord, prepositions and spelling; idioms, figures of speech and question tags for Year 10 / SSS 1.
  • General Knowledge: Nigeria's states, capitals and zones, civics, science, the world's capitals, and computing for Year 10 / SSS 1.

They are only added to the Question pool, alongside your own past papers and practice bank; they are never an examination paper and are not in Question Banks. To serve only your own questions, choose Last session's examination only or Practice bank only.

The ready-made questions mean practice works from day one. Keep each year's entrance papers (they join the pool once their session is over) and add a practice bank of your own: the pool grows, and a retake repeats less. The current session's papers are never used for practice, so practising can never reveal an examination that has not been sat yet.

Troubleshooting: the entrance examination and its practice

A practice subject says "Unavailable".
It has nothing to serve. With the Question pool this does not happen (the ready-made questions are always there), so the subject is set to Last session's examination only with no earlier paper, or to Practice bank only with no bank. Switch it back to the Question pool, or choose a practice bank.
Practisers see the same questions every time.
The pool is no bigger than one attempt (the page says "every attempt sees the same questions"). Use the Question pool (it brings the ready-made questions), add a practice bank with more questions, or lower Per attempt.
A ready-made question looks wrong.
Tell the platform operator who set up your school which one (its words, class and subject). Meanwhile, choose Practice bank only for that subject to serve only your own questions.
"That bank is used by the current entrance examination".
A bank the current session's papers use cannot also be practice. Make a separate bank for practice.
A candidate cannot start a paper: "This paper is not ready…".
The paper's bank has no questions, or no paper is set up for the current session. On Exam Configuration, set up the standard papers, or configure and activate the school's own bank.
"…not valid for a 100-point examination".
A paper is always out of 100, so the number of questions served must divide 100 evenly in hundredths: 20, 25, 40, 50 and so on. Choose such a number, or leave it blank.
"You have already taken this paper".
One attempt per paper. For a genuine second attempt, grant a retake on the candidate's page once the first is finished.
The power went off during the examination.
Answers were saved as given. Candidates reload (or sign in again on another computer) and continue. Time lost is not given back automatically: grant a retake to anyone who lost a lot.
"This question bank is locked…".
A bank is locked during a live examination so it cannot change mid-paper. The School Admin unlocks it under Settings, Controls and alerts, once the examination is over.
A candidate is not on the admissions waitlist.
A candidate joins it once they have sat at least one paper.

From candidate to student

Once a candidate has sat at least one paper, they move onto the {{ page('admissions', 'admissions waitlist') }} — where an admissions officer decides to admit them into a real, enrolled student account, or decline them.
