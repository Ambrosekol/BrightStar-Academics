Running the school itself — its staff, its roles, its look, and who did what.

The Administration page

Administration is a hub, not one page of its own content: it shows a few counts (how many staff accounts, how many roles, how many unread control items) and links into the real pages described below.

Administration is one of four areas kept for the School Admin alone — the account created when the school itself was set up, never a role you build or grant. Email & WhatsApp, Online Payments and School Profile, further down this page, are the other three. Nobody else can be given access to any of the four, no matter what permissions their role carries; if you cannot see this link at all, that is why, and it is normal for every account except the School Admin's own.

Staff accounts

Accounts lists every member of staff. From here you add a new one — a name, a username, an email, a phone number, and which role(s) they hold — or open an existing account to edit its details, change its roles or scope, switch its sign-in on or off, or reset its password to a fresh one-time password. A new account always starts on a one-time password that must be changed at first sign-in; nobody, not even the School Admin, can ever see what a password is, only reset it.

Roles

Roles is where the named bundles of permissions described in {{ page('roles-permissions', 'Roles, permissions and scope') }} are actually built and edited — pick which permissions a role carries by ticking them, from the full list.

Two further pages exist mainly for checking your own work, reached by typing their address rather than a button in the menu (so do not worry if you never notice them): the permissions catalogue lists every permission that exists in the whole system, grouped by area, so you can see exactly what a role could carry before you build one; scopes lists every account in the school that is currently limited to particular classes, subjects or banks, all in one place.

Messages

Messages is a simple inbox between members of staff — pick a colleague, write a note, attach a file if you need to. It is only ever between staff; a parent or a student never sees it and cannot use it. A small red number by the link in the navigation rail shows how many messages you have not opened yet.

Who is online now

Online Now shows which staff, students and parents are currently signed in and active — useful, for example, to know whether the person you want to message is actually at their screen right now.

Audit log

The Activity Log records who did what, to which record, from which address, and whether it succeeded — every release, every account change, every sign-in. Some actions also notify School Admins directly.

Controls and locks

Controls & Alerts lists things raised automatically that need a senior person's review, and lets a resource — a question bank during a live exam, say — be frozen against changes, with a reason, until it is unlocked.

How the portal looks

School Profile sets the school's name, motto, logo, brand colours and sign-in photographs — everything a receipt, a report card and every page of the portal wear as their own. School Admin only, like Administration above: changing what the school is called or how it looks is kept out of ordinary staff reach.

Email and WhatsApp

Email & WhatsApp is where the school connects its own mail server and WhatsApp Business account, so every automatic notice — a new report card, a payment, a released timetable — reaches a guardian on those channels too, not only in-app. School Admin only, since whoever holds these accounts can send a message that looks like it comes from the school.
