Finance covers what a student is charged, what has been paid, the receipt that proves it, and what the school spends. It is one page with tabs in three groups (each shown only to those allowed to open it): taking the money in (Collections, Payments, Student accounts, New students to bill), what is charged (Fee structure, Discounts, Instalments) and money going out (Expenses, Profit & loss), with + Record payment at the top right. Recording a payment, adding or editing a fee item, a payment and a student's account all open in a pop-up over the page you are on; Cancel, the × or a click outside closes it.

Collections: what came in, and where the fees stand

Collections opens with today's takings, this month's, and the total for the period you choose (7 days, 30 days, 90 days or 12 months) against the period before it. The chart shows every day, week or month in that period; hover over or tab to a column for its amount and number of payments, and Table under it lists the same figures. Beside it: how the money was paid (cash, bank transfer, POS, online) and, for a bursar or the School Admin, which member of staff took it.

For those who may see school-wide figures, Where the fees stand follows, for the session you pick: the share of charged fees collected so far (the striped part of the bar is owed and already past its due date), how many students have paid in full, part paid, paid nothing or have no fees yet (each a link to that list), the largest balances, and the same figures by class and by fee. A cashier sees only the money they recorded themselves, and no school-wide balance.

Student accounts

Student accounts lists every student enrolled in a session, or charged for it, with what they were charged, what has been applied to those charges, how far along they are, their balance (and how much of it is overdue) and the date of their last payment. Narrow it by class with the chips, by where students stand (Owing, Nothing paid, Part paid, Paid in full, No fees yet), or by name or admission number, and order it by largest balance, name, class, most paid or lowest share paid. Each choice loads in place. The figures across the top always add up the students shown.

Fee items

Fee Structure is where a school defines what it charges — tuition, a term's dues, a one-off charge — and which classes each applies to (+ Add fee item), charges students, and looks back over every charge made. This is the fourth step of the new-school setup checklist.

Billing a whole class

On Fee Structure, the top of the page chooses between Bill a student, for one child, and Bill a class, for everyone enrolled in a class for the session. Pick the session, the class, the billing period and, if you want one, a due date; the fees offered are the ones assigned to that class.

  • Review first. Review shows how many students will be billed, each fee and the total per student. Nothing is charged until you confirm on that screen.
  • Watch every student live. The progress window lists every student in the class and shows, as it happens, who is waiting, who is being billed now, who is billed (with the amount), who was already charged and who was not billed (with the reason). Press a count at the top, such as Waiting or Not billed, to show only those students.
  • The progress window stays open until every student on the list is confirmed as billed. If a student could not be billed, the window shows who and why; Retry the students not yet billed bills only those.
  • If the window is closed by mistake. While billing runs it has no close button and Escape does nothing, and leaving the page, pressing Back or closing the tab first asks Leave site?; choose to stay and billing carries on. If the page is left anyway (or the power or connection goes), every student is either billed in full or not at all, never halfway, and the students already billed stay billed, with their parents told. Open the class again and bill it: everyone already charged is skipped, so only the rest are billed. New students not billed yet are still on New students to bill.
  • Parents are told in the background. Their email and text go out after their child is billed, without slowing the billing down. Every message, sent or not, is in Activity & notice logs.
  • Safe to run again. A student already charged a fee for that billing period is skipped, so billing the class again after an error never charges anyone twice.
  • Archiving. Archiving or activating a fee asks first. Archiving stops it being offered for new charges; charges and payments already made are not changed.

New students to bill

New students to bill (in the menu under Finance) is the bursar's reminder: every student who joins the school appears on it on their own, whether they were admitted from the entrance exam, registered by hand or imported, and stays until they have been billed and the bill is paid. The number beside it in the menu, and New, not billed on the Overview, count those not billed yet. Students already in the school before this list arrived are not on it.

  • Not billed yet: nothing has been charged. Bill opens Fee structure with the student already chosen.
  • Bill a class's new students together. Above the list, each class with new students not billed yet has a button, such as Bill all 12 together. It opens Fee structure on Bill a class with that class chosen and only those new students on it (not the rest of the class): pick the fees once, review, and watch each student being billed. To bill just some of them, tick them and press Bill ticked students together; they must all be in one class, because the fees offered are the class's. A student with no class this session is billed on their own.
  • Waiting for payment: billed, with what has been paid and what is still owed. Record payment opens the payment form for that student. Money received but not yet applied to the fee is shown as received, not yet applied: apply it to the fee (Payments) and the student counts as paid.
  • Done: once everything billed is paid, the student leaves the list on their own and is kept here, marked Paid in full.
  • Not billing someone? (a scholarship, a staff child): tick them, say why, and press Take ticked students off the list. This needs the right to manage fees (the Finance Manager role has it); the note is kept under Done, where Put back returns them to the list. To bill them less rather than not at all, give them a discount or scholarship under Discounts (below) and bill them as usual.

Discounts, scholarships and the sibling discount

Discounts (it needs the right to manage fees, which the Finance Manager has) lists every discount given to a student, and holds the school's one sibling rule. A discount is taken off each fee as the student is billed, by a student or a whole class: the bill shows the full fee, what was taken off and why, and the student owes the rest. Receipts, balances and reminders all use what is owed.

  • + Give a discount: choose the student, the kind (Scholarship, Staff child or Discount), a percentage or a number of naira off each fee, which fees it covers (every fee, or one such as Tuition), and whether it is for this session or every session until it is ended. Tick Also take it off fees already charged to re-work this session's bills at once. A 100% scholarship is charged at nothing and shows as paid.
  • Sibling discount: switch it on, choose from the 2nd or the 3rd child, the percentage and the fees. Children are one family when a parent account is linked to each of them; the first child is the one who joined the school first. Percentages add up (never past 100%).
  • Ending a discount keeps it on the record under Ended. Fees charged from then on are charged in full; tick undo on this session's fees to charge this session's unpaid bills in full again.
  • A fee is never set below what has already been paid on it. The rest of the discount waits: the student's fee account shows it as waiting on a payment, and if that payment is voided, or refunded through Paystack, it comes off on its own. A discount ended on purpose is never brought back this way.
  • A student's own discounts are also on their fee account, where they can be given and ended.

Instalment plans

Instalments lets families pay a billing period's fees in parts, each by its own date: First Term in two parts: 60% by 15 September, 40% by 15 October. + New plan asks for a name, the session, the billing period and the parts (they must add up to 100%; the running total says so). Students puts a whole class or one student on it, and shows each one's overdue amount and next part; a student can also be put on a plan from their fee account.

A part is due by its date and overdue from the next day. Until then it is not overdue: Student accounts, the fee reminders and the report card hold all follow the plan, so a family paying on time is never chased or held back. A student on no plan is overdue by each fee's own due date, as before. Stop using a plan and its students go back to the fees' own dates.

Fee reminders

Fee reminders (Settings, Fees & payments) texts and emails the families whose fees are overdue, on its own, every 3, 7, 14 or 30 days, between 9:00 and 18:00, to families owing at least the amount you set. The words of the text and the email are edited on the same page, with the parts in curly brackets ({child}, {amount} overdue, {balance} still owed) filled in for each family and a preview as you type; the text is kept to one text message.

  • Due a reminder now lists who would be reminded today, and Send reminders now sends at once, whether reminders are on or off. A family is never reminded twice in one day.
  • On a student's fee account, Send a reminder reminds that one family, and shows when they were last reminded.
  • A balance left from an earlier session counts as overdue in full. Every message is in the notice log as Fee reminder, and parents also see it in the parent portal.

Holding report cards for unpaid fees

Report card hold (Settings, Fees & payments; the School Admin only) keeps report cards from families who owe fees. Choose only what is overdue (which follows due dates and instalment plans) or any unpaid balance, and an amount it must be above. Results are still released and staff see and print every card; only the family's copy waits. A parent sees the cards marked Held, with what is owed and a link to pay; a student is asked to speak to their parent. The cards open the moment the balance is paid.

To let one student's cards through (a promise to pay, a hardship case), open their fee account and press Let through… with a reason. Everyone let through is listed on the settings page, with Hold again.

Expenses

Expenses (the Record expenses permission, which the Finance Manager has) is what the school spends, other than staff pay: diesel, repairs, books, a vendor's bill. + Record expense takes the amount, what it was for, the date, who was paid and how, and a photo of the receipt if there is one (only finance staff can open it). The page shows one month at a time (‹ and ›), with its total against the month before and each category's total; press a category to see only those. A mistake is voided with a reason, never deleted, and stays under Voided.

Profit and loss

Profit & loss (the School Admin, or anyone given See profit and loss, since it includes the total paid in salaries) shows, for a month, a session (using its start and end dates, set under Academic sessions) or any dates: money in (fee payments, by the fee they paid, and store sales) and money out (staff pay from every payroll marked paid, with the school's pension contribution, and expenses by category), and the surplus or shortfall. A longer period also shows month by month. It counts money when it moved: fees still owed are not income until they are paid. Print gives a clean copy for the proprietor or the accountant.

Recording a payment

+ Record payment opens a short form in a pop-up: who paid (type part of the student's name or admission number to narrow the list; it shows where the receipt will be sent) and what was paid (the amount, what it is for, how it was paid, and a reference or note if there is one). Saving makes a numbered receipt at once, sends it to the guardian, and opens the payment's own page, ready to apply it to fees.

Payments lists every payment: all of them, those not yet applied to a fee, those fully applied, or voided ones, for one session or every session, by method, or found by student, admission number, receipt number or payer. A cashier sees only the payments they recorded.

A payment's own page

Opening a payment (from Payments, Collections or a student's account) shows everything about it in one place: the receipt exactly as issued, with Print receipt and Open PDF; Apply to fees; Send to the family (email the receipt, or send the payment text, again); and, for those who manage finance, Correct a mistake. A payment can be voided there, with a reason, if it was entered wrong; voiding never deletes it, it just stops it counting. Voiding is only ever a correction to the record - for a payment a parent actually made online, see Refunding an online payment below, which is the action that sends the money itself back.

Deciding what a payment covers

A payment on its own is just an amount received — applying it is what says which particular charge (this term's tuition, say, or the uniform levy) it pays off. On the payment's page, Apply to fees lists the student's fees still owing for that session, each with how it is billed and what it still owes. Type an amount against each, use Fill to give one fee as much as it owes, or Fill oldest first to pay the oldest fees until the money runs out; the running total shows what is left, and Apply saves it. A payment can cover several charges at once, or only part of one, and what is left still shows as not yet applied.

For as long as any posted payment - by any means, including one paid online - has not been allocated to a charge, every finance officer sees a banner naming how many and how much, on every page, not just Finance. It is not a message you can dismiss: it goes away on its own, the moment the last one is allocated, precisely so a payment can never be quietly forgotten.

A student's own fee account

A student's account (opened from Student accounts, from their own page under Students, or from a payment's receipt) opens in a pop-up and shows, for the session you choose: every fee charged and how it is billed (One-time, Full Session, or First, Second or Third Term), what has been paid against each and what is still owed; and every payment made in that session, with the fees it paid and any part not yet applied. Receipt opens that payment in the same pop-up, and + Record a payment starts one for this student — the same figures a linked parent sees in their own version.

The receipt

Every school's receipt carries its own branding — logo, colours, name, address, phone, a signature and, if set, the bursar's own notes — drawn from the same description on screen, in print and in the PDF a parent is sent, so all three always agree. A receipt can be re-sent by email (and the payment text by SMS), or downloaded again, at any time. Receipt settings is where that authorised signature image is uploaded, and where a school can choose its own receipt-number prefix, once, for the whole school.

Recording a payment automatically tells the parent — an in-app alert, an email with the receipt attached and a text message — the moment it is saved.

The receipt studio: the school's own receipt

The receipt studio (under Design studios, in the menu under Management, Design & print, or Design the receipt on Receipt settings) designs the school's own receipt. It needs the right to manage finance (the Finance Manager has it); a cashier who only records payments cannot open it.

  • Start from a template. Classic, Modern, Elegant or Bold, in the school's colours, on A5 landscape like today's receipt. A5 portrait and A4 either way round are under the Page tab.
  • The receipt's blocks. Amount paid (the figure as large as its box allows, the amount in words under it if wanted), Balance (fees for the session, paid so far, balance due) and a Paid stamp (round or boxed, the school's own word, the date paid). Each has its own font, sizes and colours.
  • The payment's details. Receipt number, date, who paid, the student, admission number, class, session, what it was for, how it was paid, the reference, the amount in figures and in words, who received it, the bursar's note, the balance, and the school's own details: click one under Details. And text, shapes, the logo, the authorised signature (set on Receipt settings), a barcode of the receipt number and the school's own images.
  • Preview, save, switch on. Preview PDF shows it with a made-up payment. Receipts keep the standard design until you press Use this design; from then on every receipt - the payment page, the printout, the PDF and the copy emailed to parents - is drawn from the saved design. A voided receipt is always marked VOID across the page and never shows the Paid stamp, whatever the design.

Every tool in the studio, with pictures, is explained in {{ page('design-studios', 'Design studios') }}.

Troubleshooting: receipts

The receipt has no signature.
Upload or draw the authorised signature on Receipt settings.
The balance on the receipt is empty.
No fees are charged to the student for that session. Charge them under Fee structure (or Student accounts); the balance then shows on every receipt.
Receipts still look the standard way.
Press Use this design in the receipt studio after saving. It can take up to a minute to reach every receipt.
A receipt says VOID.
The payment was voided (corrected). That is always shown, so a cancelled receipt can never pass for a paid one.
The PDF prints at the wrong size.
Print at 100% and choose the paper the design uses (A5 or A4, under the receipt studio's Page tab).
A parent did not get the receipt by email.
The student has no guardian email, or Email & SMS is not set up. The payment's page shows each attempt; send it again from there.

What a parent sees

A linked parent sees their child's full fee account: what has been charged, what has been paid, and the outstanding balance, with every receipt available to download again.

Letting a parent pay online

Online Payments connects your school's own Paystack account — enter the public and secret keys from your Paystack dashboard, and save. It is School Admin only, even for a Finance Manager, since it decides where the school's money goes. Once set up, every parent sees a "Pay online" box on their child's fee account, for any amount up to what is outstanding; they pay by card, bank transfer or however Paystack itself offers, and the payment appears in your records — with its own receipt and parent notification — automatically, the moment Paystack confirms it, exactly as if a member of staff had typed it in. There is no shared platform account behind this: until a school sets up its own, parents simply do not see the option.

Paste the webhook address shown on this page into Paystack's own dashboard. It is what lets a payment still be recorded correctly even if the parent closes their browser the moment they finish paying, before being sent back to the portal. Even without that, the system still checks on its own the next time the parent or a finance officer opens a relevant page, so a payment Paystack actually took is never simply lost to a missed webhook.

Refunding an online payment

A payment a parent made online has its own Refund via Paystack button on the receipt page, alongside Void - the same finance permission, but a different action: this one asks Paystack itself to send the money back, rather than just correcting the record. Give a reason and confirm; the payment is not marked refunded straight away, because starting a refund is not the same as Paystack having actually sent the money back yet. Once Paystack confirms it, the payment is voided automatically, with a note that it was refunded, not just corrected by hand - and if Paystack instead reports the refund failed, the payment is left exactly as it was, so nothing is ever marked refunded that did not really happen. A payment recorded by hand, or already refunded, does not offer this button - void it instead, and settle the parent outside the system, the same way as always.

Payroll

Payroll (in the menu under Management, Staff) pays the school's staff each month. The Payroll Officer role keeps people's pay, prepares each month and marks it paid; only the School Admin (the proprietor or proprietress) approves it. Salaries are seen only by those two: not by the bursar, not by HR, not by other staff. If the School Admin ever wants someone else to see or approve payroll, they give that person the permission personally.

  • Settings first. PAYE bands, rent relief, pension (8% from the employee, 10% from the school) and NHF start from the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 and the Pension Reform Act 2014. They are a starting point, not tax advice: have the school's accountant check them and put their name on the settings page. Every payroll keeps a copy of the settings it used.
  • Staff pay. For each member of staff (from Staff records): their basic salary a month, the allowances and deductions they get (Housing, Transport, Cooperative, or the school's own items), their bank, pension administrator, RSA PIN and tax ID. Bank numbers, PINs and tax IDs are kept encrypted; only the last four digits show. Nobody sets their own pay.
  • A month. Prepare works out everyone's payslip. Check them; Add a line pays arrears or a bonus, or takes back a salary advance, for that month only. Then someone other than whoever prepared it Approves it, checking the Change from column, which shows each net pay against the last paid month (a rise of more than a tenth is marked): it is locked, and staff see their payslips on My payslips. Only the School Admin approves.
  • Paying by the bank. Download the bank schedule (name, bank, account number, amount) for the bank, and the PAYE, pension and NHF returns for remitting them, then Mark paid; a paid month is final. An approved month not yet paid can be reopened, and must be approved again (after someone else has changed it).
  • Paying through Paystack. When the school's Paystack account is connected (Settings, Online payments), an approved month shows Review and pay, to the School Admin only: nobody else sends salaries, and a platform operator visiting the school cannot either. See below.
  • Unpaid leave can be taken off pay automatically (a setting): each working day of approved leave of a kind that is not paid takes a day's share of basic and allowances.
  • PAYE is worked out on each month's pay as if paid all year. If someone's pay changes during the year, the accountant settles the difference at the year's end.

Paying salaries through Paystack

The School Admin (the proprietor or proprietress) can send each person's net pay straight to their bank from the school's Paystack balance, once the month is approved.

  1. Review. Review and pay lists everyone with pay to receive: their bank, the last four digits of the account, their net pay, and the name the bank holds for that account, asked from Paystack. Check the names. Someone whose name does not match the payroll's is left unticked: tick them only if you are sure (a married name, a nickname). The school's Paystack balance is shown beside the total.
  2. Who is not offered. Someone with no bank account on their payslip; someone whose bank details were changed after the month was approved (nobody can redirect a salary after approval: reopen the month and approve it again); or someone whose bank or account Paystack cannot find. Pay them by the bank schedule.
  3. Send. Type your own password and press Send salaries. Everything is checked again first; if the balance does not cover the total, nothing is sent. Each person's payslip then shows Sending, and Paid by Paystack once the bank confirms. Nobody is ever paid twice: pressing again only sends to those not paid yet.
  4. Finished. When everyone has been paid through Paystack, the month is marked paid on its own. If you paid some people by the bank instead, the Payroll Officer marks the month paid once nothing is still being sent.
Once any salary has been sent, the month can no longer be reopened: put a mistake right in next month's payroll. Every review, payment and refusal is in the activity log; account numbers never are.

Troubleshooting: payroll

Someone is missing from the month's payroll.
They need a staff record and their pay set under Staff pay, marked as on the payroll. Add them, then prepare the month again (a month not yet approved can be prepared again).
I cannot approve the payroll.
Only the School Admin approves, and not a month they prepared or last changed themselves. A paid month is final.
I cannot change my own pay.
That is on purpose: nobody sets their own pay.
A net pay is marked as a rise.
It is more than a tenth higher than the last paid month. Check the reason (a promotion, arrears) before approving.
There is no Review and pay button.
It is for the School Admin only, on an approved month, with the school's Paystack connected under Settings, Online payments.
"…turn off the one-time code (OTP) for transfers".
Paystack asks for a code for each transfer, which stops several being sent at once. Turn off the OTP for transfers in your Paystack dashboard (Settings, Preferences, Transfers; if it is not offered there, ask Paystack support to turn it off), then send again: those not paid are offered again.
"The school's Paystack balance does not cover…".
Fund the Paystack balance (Paystack, Balance, Top up), then send again. Nothing was sent.
A payslip shows Sending for a long time.
Press Check transfers on the month: it asks Paystack about each one. A transfer Paystack has no record of after a quarter of an hour is marked not paid, and can be sent again.
A payslip shows Not paid or Sent back.
Hover over it for Paystack's reason (often a closed account). Correct the person's bank details for next month, and pay this month by the bank.
The tax figures look wrong.
Check the payroll settings with the school's accountant; every payroll keeps a copy of the settings it used.
