Every account has one or more roles, each a named bundle of individual permissions (things like "View students" or "Release timetables"). Whether you see a link in the navigation, or can open a page, or get refused with "you don't have permission" — all of it comes down to which permissions your roles give you.

The School Admin role

Every school has a School Admin role that holds every permission and is never limited to particular classes or subjects. If you can see everything described in this guide and more, you probably hold this role, or you are a platform operator visiting the school (see below).

Ready-made roles

A new school starts with seventeen ready-made roles under Roles — Primary Class Teacher, Finance Manager, Librarian, Report Card Officer, Parents' Feedback Officer and more — each already carrying a sensible bundle of permissions for that job. You can edit what a role carries, or build your own from scratch. Roles and accounts are themselves managed under {{ page('staff-admin', 'Administration') }}, which — like Email & WhatsApp, Online Payments and School Profile — is kept for the School Admin alone, so nobody can hand themselves or a colleague a permission they should not have.

Scope: limited to some classes or subjects

A class teacher is usually limited to their own class; a subject teacher can be limited to a class and a subject together; someone managing question banks can be limited to particular banks. This is set per account, from that account's own page under Accounts. It is why two teachers looking at the same page — Students, Attendance, Results — see different rows: each sees only what their scope allows.

If a page or a button you expect to see is missing, it is almost always a permission or a scope, not a fault. Ask whoever manages Administration at your school to check what your account's role and scope allow.

The bulk-import permission

The permission Run bulk imports (school.bulk_import) can be given to any role. It lets that role run all three bulk import steps (students, enrolment history and past results) without also holding each step's own permission. Its holder can also create an earlier session during the history step, and archive a graduate. It grants nothing else: the rest of the school stays as the role allows. Every import is recorded in the audit log with who ran it, the file name and the counts.

Finding a permission quickly

On a staff member's page, under Overrides, a search box filters the permissions as you type. It is forgiving: capital letters, accents, the order of words and small spelling mistakes do not matter, so "student delete" and "finence" both find what you are looking for. Overrides are for exceptions only. Ordinary access should come from roles.

New accounts and temporary passwords

A new staff, student or parent account is always created with a one-time password, shown once when the account is made. The first sign-in forces a real password to be chosen before anything else opens. Nobody — not even the School Admin — can see what that password was afterwards, only reset it to a new one-time password.
