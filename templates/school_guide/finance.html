Finance covers what a student is charged, what has been paid, and the receipt that proves it.

Fee items

Fee Structure is where a school defines what it charges — tuition, a term's dues, a one-off charge — and which classes each applies to. This is the fourth step of the new-school setup checklist.

Recording a payment

From Finance, recording a payment updates the student's balance and generates a receipt at once. A payment can be voided, with a reason, if it was entered wrong; voiding never deletes it, it just stops it counting.

Deciding what a payment covers

A payment on its own is just an amount received — allocating it is what says which particular charge (this term's tuition, say, or the uniform levy) it pays off. Open a payment from a student's account page to allocate it across one or more of that student's outstanding charges; a payment can cover several charges at once, or only part of one, and what is left unallocated still shows as credit on the account.

A student's own fee account

A student's account (opened from their own page under Students, or from a payment's receipt) lists everything ever charged to them and every payment ever recorded for them, session by session, with the running balance — the same page a linked parent sees their own version of.

The receipt

Every school's receipt carries its own branding — logo, colours, name, address, phone, a signature and, if set, the bursar's own notes — drawn from the same description on screen, in print and in the PDF a parent is sent, so all three always agree. A receipt can be re-sent by email or WhatsApp, or downloaded again, at any time. Receipt settings is where that authorised signature image is uploaded, and where a school can choose its own receipt-number prefix, once, for the whole school.

Recording a payment automatically tells the parent — an in-app alert, an email and a WhatsApp message with the receipt attached — the moment it is saved.

What a parent sees

A linked parent sees their child's full fee account: what has been charged, what has been paid, and the outstanding balance, with every receipt available to download again.
