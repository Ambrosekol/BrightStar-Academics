Attendance is taken once per student per day — not per subject — by whoever teaches that class.

Taking the register

Choose the class with the chips at the top. The day shows with the school week beside it: a green dot under a day means its register was taken, and the arrows step to the school day before or after. Each student gets one tap: Present, Late, Absent or Excused. Everyone present and The rest present save time on a normal day, and the counts across the top follow as you go. Nothing is saved until Save the register, which stays at the foot of the screen. A student's name opens their own pop-up.

Open Attendance, choose the class, session, term and date, and mark each student present, late, absent or excused. A student left blank simply has no record for that day — nothing assumes a student was present by default. Saving the same marks again changes nothing; changing a mark re-attributes it to whoever changed it, so the record always shows who last touched it.

The term summary

Term summary totals present, late, absent and excused for every student in a class over a term, with a percentage that counts late as present. It only ever counts days the register was actually taken — a day nobody marked is not counted either way.

Who can do what

Taking the register needs Mark attendance; seeing the term summary only needs View attendance. A class teacher preset carries both, limited to their own class, as described in Roles, permissions and scope.

What students and parents see

A student sees their own attendance summary and day-by-day history on their dashboard, for whichever session and term they choose; a linked parent sees the same for their child. Neither ever sees another student's record.
