This guide explains what each part of your portal is for and how the pieces connect — not the software's code, just how to get things done. It is the same guide for every school; only what you can see depends on your own account's permissions.

Two workspaces

Most staff never see a choice: unless your account has been given the entrance examination (candidates, question banks, the exam itself or its results), signing in takes you straight to the school portal. Teachers do not have the entrance workspace unless the School Admin gives it to them. Someone given only the entrance workspace goes straight there, and only someone with both sees Workspace Home and Switch workspace.

If you have both, when you sign in you land on Workspace Home, where you choose one of two workspaces: School Portal (with the current session and how many students are enrolled) or Entrance Examination (with how many candidates are registered), each with its own Enter button. You can only be in one at a time, and you switch with Switch workspace at the foot of the menu, just above your name. The menu itself is grouped: Overview and Messages first, then People (students, parents, teachers and, for JSS and SSS class teachers, class transfers), Teaching (classes, subjects, attendance, academics, the exam timetable, report cards, promotion), Finance, Store (inventory, purchase claims, recording a payment) and Resources, with Settings at the foot. You only see the entries your account may open.

  • School — everyday running of the school: classes, students, attendance, work, timetables, results, report cards, finance, parents, library.
  • Entrance Examination — the entrance exam a candidate sits before they are ever a student here: question banks, candidate registration, the exam itself, results. See {{ page('entrance', 'Entrance examinations') }}.
You cannot open a School page while inside the Entrance workspace, or the reverse — the portal stops you and sends you back to Workspace Home to choose. This is deliberate: the two are kept apart so nobody wanders from one into the other by mistake.

The portal as an app

The portal can be installed like an app on a phone, a tablet or a computer. A bar at the bottom of the screen offers Install {{ school_brand.name if school_brand is defined else 'the portal' }}; on an iPhone or iPad it says how instead (Share, then Add to Home Screen). Its × closes it for two weeks; the browser's own Install (in Chrome's and Edge's address bar) works at any time. It opens in its own window, in the school's name, with the school's logo as its icon; the logo is also the icon on every browser tab. Parents and students can install it the same way. It still needs a connection: without one it shows a short "You are offline" page, and nothing anyone has seen while signed in is kept on the device.

Whenever you open another page, in the browser or in the app, the page you are on gives way to the grey outline of a page under a small spinner, and the next page appears only once it has loaded and been drawn in full, so you can see it is working and never see a half-drawn page. Downloads such as a PDF or a CSV file open without it.

The Back button at the top of a page takes you to the page you were on before. A dashboard (the Overview) never has one: it is where Back leads, so from any page, Back goes at most as far as the Overview.

Where to start

Whichever workspace you choose, the very first page you land on is called Overview — Overview in the School workspace, Overview in the Entrance workspace. Think of it as the front porch: a handful of numbers (how many students, how many payments today, how many candidates registered) so you can tell at a glance whether anything needs your attention, with quick links into the busiest pages. The School workspace's Overview also has a Top of the Class section — the best result so far this term in each class you can see — see {{ page('results-report-cards', 'Results and report cards') }}.

The Entrance workspace's Overview has three cards above its figures: register a candidate, the question banks, and results and analytics. The School workspace's Overview puts Students and Parents beside Top of the Class, with the enrolled-students figures above them.

If your school is brand new, the setup checklist on the School workspace's Overview page walks you through the order most schools actually need: review classes, add subjects, enrol students, set up fees. It disappears on its own once everything is done, and you can bring it back with a small link if you dismiss it early.

Otherwise, use the list on the left of this guide. Each page is short and links to the real page it is about, so you never have to guess where a button lives. There is also a search box above that list — type a word (say, "receipt" or "attendance") and it searches every guide page for you, not just titles.

What's changed recently

JSS and SSS classes can be split into subdivisions (JSS 1A, JSS 2 Gold, SS 1A Science), each with its own class teacher, and class teachers move students between them through Class transfers, which the receiving class teacher approves. Read {{ page('classes-subjects', 'Classes and subjects') }}. Choosing a workspace on Workspace Home now opens it in one step.

Work can now be issued to a whole class in one step, marked work is protected from deletion, and deleted work can be restored. Read {{ page('work', 'Assignments, projects and tests') }} for those rules, and {{ page('dashboard', 'Your school dashboard') }} for the Top of the Class card and the setup checklist.

Students who have left can be archived rather than deleted, and past results can be brought in from a previous system. Read {{ page('students', 'Students') }} for archiving and the three bulk import steps, {{ page('results-report-cards', 'Results and report cards') }} for past results, and {{ page('staff-admin', 'Staff, roles and branding') }} for the staff directory and the panel at the bottom right of each page. Every change is listed in What's new in that panel, and the version you are running is shown at the bottom of the sign-in page.

A few words this guide uses a lot

You do not need to memorise these — they are explained again wherever they matter — but having them in one place helps if a word confuses you later.

  • Endpoint / route — not a word you need at all as a school staff member; ignore it if you ever see it in a technical document. Everywhere in this guide we just say "page".
  • Permission — one specific thing an account is allowed to do, like "mark attendance" or "record a payment". See {{ page('roles-permissions', 'Roles, permissions and scope') }}.
  • Scope — a limit on which permission applies to which rows, e.g. a class teacher's "mark attendance" only working for their own class, not every class in the school.
  • Session — a school year, e.g. "2025/2026". See {{ page('classes-subjects', 'Classes and subjects') }}.
  • Term — First, Second or Third Term inside a session (or "Full Session" for the whole year at once).
  • Released — made visible outside staff. A result, a report card, an exam timetable are all held back from students and parents until a member of staff with the right permission releases them.
