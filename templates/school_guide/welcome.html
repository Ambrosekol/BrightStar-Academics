This guide explains what each part of your portal is for and how the pieces connect — not the software's code, just how to get things done. It is the same guide for every school; only what you can see depends on your own account's permissions.

Two workspaces

When you sign in you land on Workspace Home, where you choose one of two workspaces. You can only be in one at a time, and you switch with Exit Workspace at the top of the navigation rail.

  • School — everyday running of the school: classes, students, attendance, work, timetables, results, report cards, finance, parents, library.
  • Entrance Examination — the entrance exam a candidate sits before they are ever a student here: question banks, candidate registration, the exam itself, results. See {{ page('entrance', 'Entrance examinations') }}.
You cannot open a School page while inside the Entrance workspace, or the reverse — the portal stops you and sends you back to Workspace Home to choose. This is deliberate: the two are kept apart so nobody wanders from one into the other by mistake.

Where to start

Whichever workspace you choose, the very first page you land on is called Overview — Overview in the School workspace, Overview in the Entrance workspace. Think of it as the front porch: a handful of numbers (how many students, how many payments today, how many candidates registered) so you can tell at a glance whether anything needs your attention, with quick links into the busiest pages. The School workspace's Overview also has a Top of the Class section — the best result so far this term in each class you can see — see {{ page('results-report-cards', 'Results and report cards') }}.

If your school is brand new, the setup checklist on the School workspace's Overview page walks you through the order most schools actually need: review classes, add subjects, enrol students, set up fees. It disappears on its own once everything is done, and you can bring it back with a small link if you dismiss it early.

Otherwise, use the list on the left of this guide. Each page is short and links to the real page it is about, so you never have to guess where a button lives. There is also a search box above that list — type a word (say, "receipt" or "attendance") and it searches every guide page for you, not just titles.

A few words this guide uses a lot

You do not need to memorise these — they are explained again wherever they matter — but having them in one place helps if a word confuses you later.

  • Endpoint / route — not a word you need at all as a school staff member; ignore it if you ever see it in a technical document. Everywhere in this guide we just say "page".
  • Permission — one specific thing an account is allowed to do, like "mark attendance" or "record a payment". See {{ page('roles-permissions', 'Roles, permissions and scope') }}.
  • Scope — a limit on which permission applies to which rows, e.g. a class teacher's "mark attendance" only working for their own class, not every class in the school.
  • Session — a school year, e.g. "2025/2026". See {{ page('classes-subjects', 'Classes and subjects') }}.
  • Term — First, Second or Third Term inside a session (or "Full Session" for the whole year at once).
  • Released — made visible outside staff. A result, a report card, an exam timetable are all held back from students and parents until a member of staff with the right permission releases them.
