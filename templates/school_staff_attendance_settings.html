Staff attendance settings

The register

No phone check is impossible to cheat: a phone's location can be faked with an app, and indoors it can be off by a hundred metres. That is why the code on the school's screen, which changes every 30 seconds, matters most; the location is a second check, and each member of staff clocks in from one phone only. Locations are never kept, only the distance.
