A parent account exists to link to one or more students — never self-registered, same as a student's own account.

Parent accounts

Parents opens on four figures: how many parent accounts there are, how many children are linked, and how many parents have no email or no phone on file (receipts, notices and texts cannot reach them). The filters and the search (a parent, a child, a username, an email or a phone) narrow the list as you type. Each parent shows their children by name and class. + Add a parent and Manage open a pop-up: their details, then the children they may see, ticked from a list you can narrow by class or name. Make a new password is in the same pop-up; the new password shows there once, ready to print.

From Parents, create an account and link it to one or more of a family's children in one step — useful for siblings, who then share the same parent account rather than getting two. A parent's password can be reset to a new one-time password at any time.

The Parents card is also on the School workspace's Overview, under People, beside Students.

A parent of a Crèche or Nursery pupil sees the child's fees (and can pay them online), the released scores and the report cards. There is no work, attendance or timetable for those classes, since the pupils work on paper and never sign in themselves.

Messages with parents

Conversations with parents live on the Parents tab of Messages. A parent can send a question or feedback from their own portal; or a member of staff can reach a student's parent first with New message — search for the student, choose the parent, and write. The parent is told by email and SMS and replies from their portal.

Any administrator who may manage parent messages can answer any conversation they can see. The conversation shows who is currently responding (whoever replied last, or whoever pressed I'll respond), and every reply carries its writer's name. Each conversation is marked Open, In progress or Resolved; conversations waiting for an answer are listed first.

What a parent is told, automatically

A linked parent is told, without anyone at the school having to remember to tell them, whenever:

  • new work is set, or work is marked;
  • a fee is charged, or a payment is recorded (with the receipt);
  • a term's report card becomes ready;
  • an exam or test timetable is released.

Each of these goes out as an in-app alert on the parent's own dashboard, and — where the school has set up its own mail server and SMS account under Email & SMS — by email and SMS too.

A child who has left the school

When a student is archived, their own sign-in stops working. Their parents keep full access to their record on the parent portal: the academic record, the report cards and the fee account stay as they were.

What's new for parents and students

Parents and students see a short "What's new" notice on their own dashboard when the portal has changes that concern them. Each notice is complete on its own and never links to the staff guide, which families cannot open. A parent sees changes written for parents, and a student sees changes written for students. Closing the notice hides it in that browser for that account.
