This statement is written to align with the principles of Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023 and the NDPR, and with the same general principles (lawfulness, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity and confidentiality) that similar laws elsewhere share.

1. Who we are, and who this covers

Brightstars Academics is a software platform used by schools to run entrance examinations, day-to-day school administration, student and parent portals, and fee collection. Each school that uses it — its staff, students, parents, guardians and entrance-examination candidates — is covered by this statement. If you are a parent, student or staff member, your school is the organisation you should contact first about your own information (see section 9); we support your school in answering you.

2. Controller and processor

For the personal data a school enters about its own students, parents, staff and candidates, the school is the data controller: it decides what is collected and why, and it is the organisation a parent or staff member should approach first with a question or a request about their own data. Brightstars Academics acts as the school's data processor: we provide and operate the software the school uses to store and process that data, under the school's instructions, and we do not sell it, share it with other schools, or use it for anything beyond running the platform and supporting the school.

4. Why it is processed

  • To run the school's entrance examinations, academic records, report cards, assignments and results.
  • To record and reconcile fees and payments, and to produce receipts.
  • To send parents and guardians the notices they would otherwise be told by phone or letter: a payment received, a report card ready, new work set for their child.
  • To let the right staff, and only the right staff, see and change the records their role covers, and to keep a record of who changed what.
  • To keep the service itself running securely: sign-in, rate-limiting against guessing, and diagnosing a fault.

5. Legal basis

Personal data is processed under the contract between a school and Brightstars Academics, for the school's own legitimate interest in running its administration (and, for its staff and the platform's own security logs, the platform's legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and dependable), and, for anything a person is separately asked to agree to (such as a marketing communication, where that ever applies), their consent.

6. How your school's data is kept apart

Every school on this platform has its own separate database and its own separate folder of files — not a shared table filtered by a school identifier, but a database of its own. A query made for one school's data can never accidentally return another's, because the software is built so that no database connection is ever shared between two schools within the same request. A cookie or sign-in issued by one school's address is rejected if it is ever presented at another school's address. Uploaded files (student and staff photographs, signatures, question images) are served only to the people the record says may see them, checked on every request.

7. Security measures

  • Passwords are never stored in readable form; they are hashed, and changing a password immediately ends every other sign-in on that account.
  • A school's own email server password and SMS API token, where it sets one up, are encrypted before they are stored, under a key specific to that school; they are never shown again once saved, and a copy of the database does not expose them.
  • Sign-in attempts, password-recovery requests and test-message buttons are rate-limited against guessing.
  • Significant actions — creating or changing a record, a staff member's access, a payment — are written to an audit trail recording who did it, when, and from where.
  • The platform loads no advertising or analytics scripts, fonts or images from outside its own servers; its security policy blocks pages from embedding anything foreign.
  • Traffic to the platform is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

8. Retention and deletion

A school's data is kept for as long as the school's account with the platform remains active, or for as long as applicable law requires records of that kind to be kept, whichever is longer. A school may ask us at any time for an export of its own data, or for its data to be deleted once its account ends, subject to any legal or regulatory requirement to retain particular records for a set period (for example, financial records). A parent, student or staff member should raise a request to see, correct or remove their own information with their school first, as the school is best placed to act on it; the school may in turn ask us to carry it out on the platform.

9. Rights over your data

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have the right to ask to see the personal data held about you, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have it deleted, to object to certain processing, or to receive a copy of it in a portable form. Because your school is the controller of its own records, the quickest route is to ask your school directly; if you contact us instead, we will pass your request to the relevant school and support them in answering it.

10. Children's data

Much of what a school records is about children. A school is responsible, as controller, for having a proper basis to collect and hold information about the students in its care, in the way it already does for paper records; the platform's role is to hold and process that information securely on the school's behalf, under the same isolation and security measures described above, and to make it visible only to the people the school's own settings say may see it — never to another school, and never to the platform's own staff, other than someone the school has itself entered as an administrator.

11. Cookies and tracking

The platform sets one cookie: a signed session cookie that records who is signed in. It carries no advertising identifier and is not read by any third party. The platform runs no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking scripts of any kind, on any page, for anyone.

12. Sub-processors and delivery channels

Two kinds of message leave the platform on a school's behalf, and only when that school has set them up (or is using the platform's own shared account, where offered, as a stand-in until it sets up its own):

  • Email, sent through the mail server the school (or the platform, as a fallback) has configured.
  • SMS text messages, sent through BulkSMS Nigeria, using the school's own account where it has one (or the platform's, where the platform provides it). A text carries only a short notice, never a result or a balance.
