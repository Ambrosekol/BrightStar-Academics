SCHOOL PORTAL / COMMUNICATION

Email & SMS

Payment receipts, password-recovery emails and alerts about school work are sent to parents from these accounts. Use your school's own so that messages come from the school. Parents get a text message only for the moments that matter: a report card released, a new fee, a payment received, the first message when the school starts a conversation, and, if you switch it on, their child's birthday. Every text is kept to one message, and the words of every text and email can be changed under Message wording. Passwords and tokens are stored encrypted and are never shown again.
