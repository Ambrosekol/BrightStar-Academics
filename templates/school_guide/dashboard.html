The School portal's home page is the first thing you see after choosing the School workspace. It gives the school's numbers, the next steps for a new school, which term's top students each class has, and the panel that tells you what has changed. You can reach every area of the school from its cards.

The numbers at the top

The large tile counts the students enrolled in the current session. The smaller tiles count classes, subjects, assignments, projects, tests and examinations. Each one is a live count, so it changes as work is added or removed. Deleted work is not counted.

Finish setting up your school

A new school has four steps to finish before it can really run: review its classes (the standard list is already there, so usually this is done), add its subjects, enrol its students, and set up its fee items. Each step shows as done when the school has the data for it, and it links straight to the page where that happens. See {{ page('classes-subjects', 'Classes and subjects') }}, {{ page('students', 'Students') }} and {{ page('finance', 'Fees, payments and receipts') }}.

Closing the checklist hides it for the whole school, as it always has. Its progress is refreshed about every fifteen minutes, so a step you have just finished may take that long to show as done.

Top of the Class

This card shows the highest-scoring student in each class for the current term and session. A class appears only once its report card results for that term are released, so a class with no released results is left off rather than shown empty. The leader is featured at the top, and the other classes follow with their percentage and grade. The term and session are shown in the card's heading.

The score is the same one on the student's report card, so the two always agree. Changing a result changes the card once it is recalculated.

The panel at the bottom right

The panel holds the setup checklist and the What's new notices. It opens minimised, so it does not cover the page when you arrive. Read more about it in {{ page('staff-admin', 'Staff, roles and branding') }}, under The panel at the bottom right.

Where to go from here

The cards below the numbers are grouped by area: people and access, academic structure, assessment, and records and communication. Each card opens its page and says what that page is for. If a card you expect is missing, the reason is almost always a permission. See {{ page('roles-permissions', 'Roles, permissions and scope') }}.
