Finance covers what a school charges, what it has received, and the paper (or PDF) that proves it. Routes: {{ file('blueprints/finance/routes.py') }}. Rules, balance arithmetic and receipts: {{ file('blueprints/finance/helpers.py') }}. Tables: {{ file('models/finance.py') }}.

The four things

ThingTableMeaning
Fee item{{ table('finance_fee_items') }}, {{ table('finance_fee_item_classes') }}A line in the school's fee catalogue: "Tuition (JSS 1)", amount, category, applicability (Full Session, a term, or One-time), required or optional, and which classes it applies to.
Fee assessment{{ table('finance_fee_assessments') }}A fee charged to one student for one session (and term): "this child owes this much for this". Created from fee items on the assess form.
Payment{{ table('finance_payments') }}Money received: amount, category, method, reference, who paid, the date, who recorded it, an optional note, and a status (posted or voided).
Allocation{{ table('finance_payment_allocations') }}How much of a payment is applied to which assessment. Recording a payment does not apply it: allocating is a separate step.

What "paid" means

"Paid" for a fee always means allocated to that specific fee. A fee is Paid, Part Paid or Unpaid computed to the kobo (_payment_status) so a floating-point crumb never flips a status. A payment that has been recorded but not yet allocated appears as an unallocated credit in the student's totals, so a just-made payment is never invisible, and never silently hides debt on an unrelated fee. Balances are summed across every session, so a debt left from last year is not hidden when the school moves on. See _finance_student_outstanding and _finance_student_lifetime_totals.

Money is entered as naira and kept to the kobo. _money refuses NaN, infinity and absurd sizes; the amount in words on a receipt stops at the billions.

Receipt numbers

Each payment gets a number <PREFIX>-<year>-<5 digits> ({{ file('blueprints/finance/helpers.py') }}, _next_receipt_no). The prefix is a per-school setting (defaulting to the school's code) so a school keeps numbering the same way for ever. Numbers are unique per school (a database constraint), never shared or borrowed across schools.

Voiding

A wrong payment is voided with a reason, never deleted. Its receipt stays in the audit history, stays viewable, is marked VOIDED in big letters on the page, the printout, the PDF, and the parent's copy, is never emailed or sent by SMS, and its allocations stop counting. Permission: finance.manage. Voiding never moves money on its own - for a payment made online, see Refunding an online payment, below, which is the action that actually asks Paystack to send it back.

The receipt: one design per school

A receipt is drawn in the school's own identity: its two brand colours, logo (or its initials if it has none), name, motto, address, phone and email, its authorised signature image, the name of the member of staff who recorded the payment, the note the bursar attached, the student's name, admission number and class, the session, the method and reference, the amount in figures and in words, and a small account summary (fees charged, paid to date and balance due as at that payment, so re-printing an old receipt never shows a balance later payments have changed).

Telling parents

Recording a payment ({{ route('admin_finance_record') }}) automatically:

  1. adds an in-app alert for the child's linked parent accounts (_notify_parents_payment_recorded in {{ file('core/notifications.py') }});
  2. emails the guardian the receipt as a PDF with amount, date, purpose, balance and the bursar's note in the body; and
  3. sends a short payment confirmation by SMS (BulkSMS Nigeria).

Steps 2 and 3 run as the durable 'send_payment_receipt' job ({{ file('core/jobs.py') }}) via _send_payment_receipt_to_guardian, and every attempt is written to {{ table('finance_delivery_logs') }}, so the bursar can see on the receipt page whether it arrived and why not. Sending by hand (Send by email, Send text (SMS)) uses the same senders and logs the same way; who may send is finance.receipt.send.

Contact details come from the student's record (guardian_email, guardian_phone). Phone numbers are converted to international form (_ng_phone). SMS goes through BulkSMS Nigeria and costs the account that sends it credit, which is why only four moments send one (see Messages with parents).

Online payments (Paystack)

A school connects its own Paystack account under Online Payments ({{ route('admin_finance_paystack_settings') }}); there is no shared platform account, so a school with nothing saved simply has no "Pay online" option. Everything lives in {{ file('core/payments.py') }} and {{ file('blueprints/finance/paystack.py') }}; the secret key is encrypted at rest the same way {{ file('core/delivery.py') }} encrypts a mail password, under its own key-derivation domain (a compromise of one says nothing about the other), in {{ table('school_payment_settings') }}.

A parent starting a payment from {{ route('parent_child_finance') }} writes a {{ table('finance_online_payments') }} row ('pending', with a reference this application generates) before Paystack is ever asked to open a transaction, then is redirected to whatever address Paystack's own transaction/initialize gives back. Two independent things can report what happened next: Paystack redirecting the browser to {{ route('parent_finance_pay_callback') }}, and Paystack calling {{ route('paystack_webhook') }} directly, server to server (checked against its HMAC-SHA512 signature, computed with the school's own secret key, before anything in its body is read as fact). Both paths call the same _finalize, which asks Paystack's own transaction/verify/<reference> what really happened — never trusting the callback's query string or the webhook's body on their own — and a conditional UPDATE ... WHERE status = 'pending' is the actual claim that lets only the first of the two arrivals act on one reference.

A confirmed online payment becomes an ordinary {{ table('finance_payments') }} row, through the same fields a manually recorded payment gets (method='Paystack', the reference, Paystack's own confirmed amount — never what the parent's request claimed), and reuses the exact same 'send_payment_receipt' job. FinancePayment.recorded_by is not nullable — no member of staff acted on this particular payment, so it is attributed to whoever last configured the school's own Paystack settings, which is an honest answer to "who is responsible for this channel existing" rather than a claim that they typed it in.

Starting a payment is @idempotent_write('parent.pay_online') ({{ file('core/idempotency.py') }}): a resubmitted click is sent to the same Paystack checkout address again rather than opening a second transaction for the same click. The reference itself (new_reference) uses a hyphen, never an underscore, before the random part — Paystack's own API only allows -, ., = and alphanumeric characters in a transaction reference.

Closing the gap neither path alone can close: a parent who pays by bank transfer or USSD can close the tab before the callback loads, and a webhook can fail to reach a school that never pasted the address into its Paystack dashboard, or whose address changed. When both miss, Paystack has the money but nothing here would otherwise ever ask about it again. reconcile_pending ({{ file('blueprints/finance/paystack.py') }}) closes that gap the same way {{ file('core/jobs.py') }}'s own stuck-job retry does: called opportunistically from {{ route('admin_finance_dashboard') }} and {{ route('parent_child_finance') }} — ordinary page loads a finance admin or the paying parent already makes — it re-asks Paystack about any online payment still 'pending' after two minutes, rather than a separate scheduled service of its own to keep running and to fail in its own new ways.

Refunding an online payment

A payment made through Paystack has its own Refund via Paystack button on its receipt page ({{ route('admin_finance_payment_refund') }}, permission finance.manage - the same one voiding needs), for a payment still posted whose {{ table('finance_online_payments') }} row shows it really was confirmed online; a manually recorded payment offers Void instead, since there is no Paystack transaction behind it to refund. Requesting one calls Paystack's own POST /refund (request_refund, {{ file('core/payments.py') }}) and writes a {{ table('finance_refunds') }} row as 'pending' - Paystack accepting the request is not the same as the money having moved, the same distinction a payment itself draws above.

Only Paystack's own refund.processed/refund.failed webhook (also read at {{ route('paystack_webhook') }}, alongside the payment-confirmation event) settles it, through _finalize_refund ({{ file('blueprints/finance/paystack.py') }}): a conditional UPDATE ... WHERE status = 'pending' claims it into an intermediate 'settling' status first - the same shape _finalize uses for a payment - so a webhook and a reconciliation poll landing at the same moment can never both act on it. 'processed' is the one moment the original {{ table('finance_payments') }} row is voided, with a void reason that says it was refunded rather than corrected by hand; 'failed' leaves the payment exactly as it was, since no money actually moved. A school whose webhook was never set up is not stuck either: reconcile_pending_refunds asks Paystack directly (fetch_refund, GET /refund/:id) for anything still 'pending' after two minutes, called opportunistically from {{ route('admin_finance_dashboard') }} exactly the way reconcile_pending already covers a stuck payment.

A payment nobody has said what it is for

A posted payment — recorded by hand or confirmed by Paystack — does not by itself say which fee it settles; that is still a deliberate second step ({{ route('admin_finance_payment_allocate') }}). _finance_unallocated_summary and _finance_unallocated_payments ({{ file('blueprints/finance/helpers.py') }}) find every posted payment whose amount exceeds its non-voided allocations, scoped to one officer's own takings for {{ perm('finance.record') }} without {{ perm('finance.view_all') }}, the whole school otherwise. Whenever that count is above zero, every matching administrator sees it two ways: a banner injected by inject_csrf_token (app.py) on every admin page, not only Finance's own, with no button to dismiss it — it disappears on its own, the request after the last one is allocated — and {{ route('admin_finance_unallocated') }}, a dedicated list the banner links to.

Billing a whole class

The Fee Structure page can bill every student enrolled in a class for a session, not only one student at a time. The page loads the class's students from {{ route('admin_finance_class_roster') }}, which also reports, for each fee, how many of them already have it for the billing period. It then bills them in batches through {{ route('admin_finance_class_assessments_batch') }}: at most 100 students per request, 50 per batch from the page.

  • Each student is committed on their own. A batch that fails part way leaves the students before the failure billed and the rest not; nothing is half-written for one student.
  • Idempotent. Every fee item a student already has live for the billing period is skipped, so billing the class again - after an error, say - never charges anyone twice. The per-student rules are the same as the single-student route's: the student must be enrolled in that class for the session, and each fee must be assigned to that class.
  • The progress window closes only when every student is confirmed. The page confirms a student from the server's own per-student result, never from the request having returned. A retry sends only the students not yet confirmed. A student who could not be billed is listed with the reason.
  • Notifications and audit. Parents are told, through the same channels as a single charge, after each student's own commit; a failure there never stops the charge. The batch writes one audit entry, finance_class_assessment_batch, for each batch that billed anyone.
  • Permission. Both endpoints need the same finance.manage permission as the single-student route.

Permissions

  • {{ perm('finance.view_all') }} sees every payment; {{ perm('finance.view_own') }} sees only payments the person recorded (a cashier).
  • {{ perm('finance.record') }} records payments, allocates them, and reads the unallocated-payments list. {{ perm('finance.manage') }} manages fee items, voids or refunds payments, and sets the receipt signature.
  • {{ perm('finance.receipt.send') }} sends a receipt by email or SMS.
  • Setting up, testing and removing the school's own Paystack account is restricted to the school's own top-level administrator ({{ route('admin_finance_paystack_settings') }}) - not a permission any other role can be granted, since it decides where the school's money goes.
