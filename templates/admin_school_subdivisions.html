How subdivisions work examples

LettersJSS 1 with 90 students, in three subdivisions:JSS 1AJSS 1BJSS 1C
NamesAny name the school uses, such as gems or houses:JSS 2 GoldJSS 2 Diamond
By department (SSS)A subdivision can be kept to one department:SS 1A (Science)SS 1B (Science)SS 1C (Art)

The class stays one class for subjects, subject teachers, timetable, fees and results. A subdivision's class teacher takes its register, writes its report card comments and releases its results. Students are placed in turn, into the subdivision with the fewest students (in SSS, one of their department's or one with no department). Give each subdivision its class teacher under Teachers.
