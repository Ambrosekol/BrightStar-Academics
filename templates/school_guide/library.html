Library keeps books, who has borrowed what, and when it is due back.

Books

Add a book with its title, author, ISBN and however many copies the school owns; editing it can add or remove copies. A book with any copy currently on loan cannot be archived — return every copy first.

Issuing and returning

A book can be issued to a student or to a staff member, for however many days the school allows, which sets its due date. Returning it makes that copy available again immediately.
