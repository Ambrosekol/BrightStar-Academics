Does this sound familiar?

Running a school shouldn't feel like running after paper.

Most schools we talk to are held together by spreadsheets, notebooks and phone calls. It works, until term ends, or exam day arrives, or a parent asks a simple question.

Results live in spreadsheets

Marks are typed, retyped and added up by hand. One wrong cell and a child's grade is wrong. Nobody is sure which copy is the latest.

Receipts written by hand

Books run out, handwriting is hard to read, and "did we ever receive this payment?" takes an afternoon to answer.

Parents keep phoning the office

"Are the results out?" "What do I still owe?" The same questions, every term, and every answer costs someone's time.

Entrance exam day is a scramble

Printing papers, invigilating, marking by hand, ranking on a calculator, and hoping nothing gets lost.

Everyone can see everything

When one login opens every record, a mistake, or a curious visitor, can reach children's information they should never see.

A system that looks like nobody's school

Generic software with someone else's name on it. Your parents want to see your school.

There is a calmer way to run a school. Everything in one place, under your name.

Entrance examinations

Admissions day without the paperwork.

Candidates sit their papers on a computer, with a countdown clock. When they finish, the results are already marked and the ranking is already done.

  • Marked instantly. No scripts to collect, no marking marathon, no arithmetic mistakes.
  • Fair by design. Each candidate gets their questions in a different order. The clock is kept by the system, not the candidate's device.
  • Start with ours, or bring yours. Every new school comes with a standard set of entrance question banks. Add your own whenever you like.
  • A practice link for everyone. Families choose the class they are aiming for and practise, with no sign-up. It uses last year's paper or questions you set for practice, and it never shows the paper about to be sat.
  • Nothing is lost mid-exam. Every answer is saved the moment it is chosen, and a candidate in the middle of a paper carries on where they were, even if the system is restarted.
Results and report cards

From marks to report card, with nobody chasing.

Teachers enter marks. The system adds them up the way your school does it, and holds each result until the right person has checked it.

  • Exam plus continuous assessment. Tests, assignments and projects are worked into one clear result out of 100, the same way for every child.
  • Checked before it is seen. A result is entered, verified, approved, then released. Different people can do each step, so mistakes are caught before parents see them.
  • One button for a child's term. Release everything for a student and a term at once, with a confirmation so nothing goes out by accident.
  • Report cards that look like yours. Your logo and colours, the class teacher's comment with their signature, the head's signature, the class average. They are ready the moment the term is complete.
  • Parents told for you. When the report card is ready, parents get an email and a WhatsApp message.
Fees and receipts

Receipts that look like your school's own.

Record a payment and the parent has their receipt within moments, in your colours, with your logo and signature. Nobody has to remember to send it.

  • Fees set once, by class. Tuition, uniform, transport: set them per class and term, then charge students in a few clicks.
  • Always know who owes what. Paid, part paid or unpaid, per fee and per child, with any balance from earlier sessions still visible.
  • A receipt worth keeping. Your colours and logo, your address and phone, the amount in words, the bursar's note, the balance still due, and an authorised signature.
  • Sent by itself. Once your school's email and WhatsApp are connected, the parent receives the receipt by email and WhatsApp as soon as the payment is recorded.
  • Mistakes are corrected, not hidden. A wrong payment is voided with a reason, and stays in the record.
Your school, walled off

Your records are yours alone.

Many schools share one system with many others. Brightstars Academics is built the other way round: every school is a separate place, with its own address and its own private store of records.

  • Your own address. Your school gets a web address the moment it is created, and can use your own domain too.
  • Your own private records. Students, results, fees and photographs are kept apart from every other school's, not just hidden behind a filter.
  • Your own look. Your name, logo, two brand colours and up to eight photographs of your school. The Brightstars logo never appears on your portal.
  • Your own messages. Email and WhatsApp go out from your school's own accounts, so parents recognise who is writing.
  • Your own rules. You decide how student numbers and candidate codes are written.

Private by design

Children's information deserves care.

Behind the friendly screens, the platform is built to protect the people in it.

Every school on its own

Each school has its own private records and its own files. One school can never see another's, and a sign-in from one school does not work at another.

Only what people need to see

Roles decide who can enter marks, approve results, take payments or read records. Parents can open only their own children.

A record of who did what

Important actions are logged with who did them and when, so you can always answer "how did this change?"

Careful with passwords

New accounts start with a one-time password that must be changed. Repeated wrong guesses are slowed down. Changing a password signs the old one out everywhere.

Exams that survive a hiccup

Answers are saved as they are given, and time is kept by the system. A candidate in the middle of a paper is not thrown out by a restart.

Your messages, your name

Emails and WhatsApp messages leave from your school's own accounts. The sender details are stored encrypted.

Questions

Things schools ask us.

Do we need to be technical to use it?

No. Staff use ordinary web pages, and each person sees only the screens that matter to their job. Setting up your school is done with you, and you can change your colours, logo and roles yourself afterwards.

Can we use our own web address?

Yes. Your school gets an address the moment it is created, and it keeps working. If you would like your own domain as well, you add one simple record with your domain provider and we connect it.

Will parents receive messages from us or from Brightstars?

From you. Emails and WhatsApp messages leave from your school's own accounts once you connect them, so parents recognise who is writing. The Brightstars logo is never shown on your portal.

What happens on entrance exam day if something goes wrong?

Every answer is saved the moment it is chosen, and the clock is kept by the system rather than the candidate's device. A candidate who is mid-paper carries on where they were, even if the system is restarted. Staff can also grant a retake when it is needed.

Do the practice tests count towards a student's grade?

Never. Practice is only for preparing: students can take it as often as they like, the questions come in a new order each time, and nothing they do there is recorded or counted.

Can families practise for the entrance exam without registering?

Yes. Anyone with your school's practice link chooses the class they are aiming for and starts practising. It uses last year's paper or questions you have set for practice, and it never uses the paper that is about to be sat.

Can different staff members have different access?

Yes. You choose what each role can do, and you can limit a teacher to their own class. Nobody can give someone more access than they have themselves.

Can we bring our own questions?

Yes. Every new school starts with a standard set of entrance question banks written for the platform, and you can import your own question files whenever you like.

What does it cost?

Talk to us. We will look at the size of your school and what you need, and give you a clear answer.
