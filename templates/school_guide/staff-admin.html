Running the school itself — its staff, its roles, its look, and who did what.

The top bar

Along the top of every page: Guide and Messages (with a count of unread messages), the notifications bell, and your own name and photo. Click your name to open a small menu with Sign out. The name at the top is where to look to sign out; the side menu no longer has it.

The side menu

The menu opens while you hover over it. A small box on its right edge, at the bottom of the screen, keeps it open: click it once and the menu stays open on every page until you click it again. While the menu is open from hovering, that box steps aside so it never covers an item.

The Administration page

Administration is a hub, not one page of its own content: it shows a few counts (how many staff accounts, how many roles, how many unread control items) and links into the real pages described below.

Administration is one of four areas kept for the School Admin alone — the account created when the school itself was set up, never a role you build or grant. Email & WhatsApp, Online Payments and School Profile, further down this page, are the other three. Nobody else can be given access to any of the four, no matter what permissions their role carries; if you cannot see this link at all, that is why, and it is normal for every account except the School Admin's own.

Staff accounts

Accounts is a directory of everyone who can sign in to the school's administration. Each person has a card showing their name, username, whether they are active or suspended, their roles, their contact details and their last sign-in. The card also says in plain words what they can reach: full access for the School Admin, limited to a number of access areas, or standard access from their roles.

  • Search by name, username or email, and switch between Everyone, Active and Suspended.
  • Manage opens the person's own page.
  • The ⋯ menu on a card sends them a message, resets their login (they get a new one-time password), or suspends or reactivates their access. Suspending stops their sign-in straight away; their record stays, and they can be reactivated at any time. You cannot suspend or reset your own access.

A person's own page has four sections, listed down the side. Profile holds their name, contact details and photo; the username cannot be changed once created. Job roles is where you tick every role they do, with a summary that updates as you tick. Where they work leaves them with access to the whole school, or limits them to chosen classes, subjects, sessions or question banks. Overrides (shown to the School Admin) grants individual permissions for exceptions only. The bar at the bottom saves your changes, cancels, or resets their login.

A new account always starts on a one-time password that must be changed at first sign-in. Nobody, not even the School Admin, can see a password, only reset it to a new one-time password.

Roles

Roles is where the named bundles of permissions described in {{ page('roles-permissions', 'Roles, permissions and scope') }} are actually built and edited — pick which permissions a role carries by ticking them, from the full list.

Two further pages exist mainly for checking your own work, reached by typing their address rather than a button in the menu (so do not worry if you never notice them): the permissions catalogue lists every permission that exists in the whole system, grouped by area, so you can see exactly what a role could carry before you build one; scopes lists every account in the school that is currently limited to particular classes, subjects or banks, all in one place.

Messages

Messages is a simple inbox between members of staff — pick a colleague, write a note, attach a file if you need to. It is only ever between staff; a parent or a student never sees it and cannot use it. A small red number by the link in the navigation rail shows how many messages you have not opened yet.

Who is online now

Online Now shows which staff, students and parents are currently signed in and active — useful, for example, to know whether the person you want to message is actually at their screen right now.

Audit log

The Activity Log records who did what, to which record, from which address, and whether it succeeded — every release, every account change, every sign-in. Some actions also notify School Admins directly.

A link from that page, or from a student's own record, opens the Notice log — every attempt to tell a guardian something by email or WhatsApp (a new assignment, a fee charged, a released report card or exam timetable), whether it actually went through or not, so you can answer "did she get it?" yourself. Payment receipts keep their own version of this on each receipt's own page.

Controls and locks

Controls & Alerts lists things raised automatically that need a senior person's review, and lets a resource — a question bank during a live exam, say — be frozen against changes, with a reason, until it is unlocked.

How the portal looks

School Profile sets the school's name, motto, logo, brand colours and sign-in photographs — everything a receipt, a report card and every page of the portal wear as their own. School Admin only, like Administration above: changing what the school is called or how it looks is kept out of ordinary staff reach.

Email and WhatsApp

Email & WhatsApp is where the school connects its own mail server and WhatsApp Business account, so every automatic notice — a new report card, a payment, a released timetable — reaches a guardian on those channels too, not only in-app. School Admin only, since whoever holds these accounts can send a message that looks like it comes from the school.

The panel at the bottom right

Every administrator sees a small panel at the bottom right of each page, with up to two cards.

  • What's new lists the latest changes to the portal in plain language, each with a link to the guide page that explains it. It shows only the changes you have not yet closed. Closing it hides those changes for you in this browser; another person sees their own.
  • Set up your school shows the setup steps for a new school (classes, subjects, students and fees) with their progress. Closing it hides it for the whole school, as it always has.
  • Minimise folds a card to its title bar. Your choice is remembered in this browser.

The panel opens minimised for What's new, so it does not cover the page when you arrive; click its title to read it. Each card has a capped height, and a long list scrolls inside the card rather than pushing the panel over the page. The setup checklist opens expanded for a new school.

The version of the portal you are running is shown at the bottom of the sign-in page, under the sign-in form. It is read from the release notes, the same source as What's new, so the number on the sign-in page always matches the newest release listed in What's new.

So that the system stays fast for every school, the panel does not look anything up each time you open a page. What's new is kept in your browser. The setup progress is kept in your browser too, and refreshed about every fifteen minutes, so a step you finish can take up to that long to show as done.
