Upgrade schemas

Bring one school's tables up to date, or every school's at once. Upgrading every school also records a new launch (everyone signed in must sign in again, except people mid-exam) — the same as a normal deploy step.

Record a new launch

Sweep stuck jobs

Give every stuck background job in one school (or every school) another try. Safe to run any time — ordinary traffic already does this opportunistically; this is only needed for a school quiet enough that nothing else would.
