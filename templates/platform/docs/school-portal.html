Everything a school's staff use, at the school's own address. This page explains who staff are, what they are allowed to do, and how that is enforced. The list of every permission and role is generated on the permissions reference.

Two workspaces

A staff member signs in and lands on the workspace home ({{ route('admin_workspace_home') }}), where they choose Entrance Examination or School. The choice is remembered in the session and enforced by a request hook in {{ file('app.py') }} (enforce_admin_workspace_boundary): the paths of one workspace are refused from the other, so a person cannot wander from entrance administration into student records by editing an address. Which paths belong to which workspace is one function, _admin_workspace_for_path.

The chooser's two doors open each workspace's home page directly, with ?enter=school or ?enter=entrance: the same hook chooses the workspace on the way in, so entering is one request rather than a redirect through {{ route('select_school_workspace') }} or {{ route('select_entrance_workspace') }} first (those addresses still work). enter is honoured only on a GET of that workspace's own home page, and the entrance workspace still needs the person to have it. Someone with only one workspace never sees the chooser: {{ route('admin_workspace_home') }} sends them straight in.

Roles, permissions and scope

Permission
a single ability, e.g. school.results.release→ Role
a named bundle of permissions→ Account
one or more roles, plus optional overrides→ Scope
limits it to certain classes, subjects or banks
  • The catalogue. Every permission is defined once in ADMIN_PERMISSION_DEFS ({{ file('core/security.py') }}). At every start, init_admin_security in {{ file('app.py') }} seeds the catalogue into the school's {{ table('permissions') }} table without ever creating an account.
  • The School Admin role. The system role (admin_types.is_system = 1) has every permission and is unrestricted by scope. It is found by that flag, never by name: a custom role that happens to be called "School Admin" must not be mistaken for it and handed everything. Platform operators who enter a school hold this role.
  • Role presets. A new school gets nineteen ready-made roles (Head Teacher, Class Teacher, Subject Teacher, Finance Manager, Librarian, Report Card Officer and so on), seeded once; edits a school makes are kept. Each role has a level (executive, head teacher, class teacher, subject teacher) below the School Admin, and a teacher's teaching duties ({{ table('teaching_duties') }}: class teacher of a class or of one of its subdivisions, or a subject in a class and, in SSS, a department) decide which classes, subjects and students they reach. Class subdivisions and transfers are on {{ page('academics', 'Academics') }}.
  • Administration is the School Admin role's own. Creating, editing, deactivating or resetting another administrator's account, and managing roles, permissions and scopes themselves ({{ file('blueprints/administration/routes.py') }}) all require is_school_admin directly - the same gate as Academic Sessions and Promotion - rather than a grantable permission, so this is never delegated to a custom role. admin_can_delegate_roles still exists in the account-creation form's own code as a second check, but is now unreachable in practice since nothing past the school-admin gate can be a non-system account.
  • Overrides. An individual account can carry permission overrides independent of role ({{ table('admin_permissions') }}).
  • Scope. A class teacher is limited to their own class; a college subject teacher can additionally be limited to a subject; a bank manager to particular question banks. Scopes are rows in {{ table('admin_scopes') }} and are checked by admin_scope_allows. Denials are audited (scope_denied).

How a route is guarded

@app.route('/admin/school/results')
@admin_required
def admin_school_results(): ...

admin_required ({{ file('core/security.py') }}) looks up the route's endpoint name in ADMIN_ENDPOINT_PERMISSIONS, checks the signed-in account holds that permission (falling back to admin.access for an unmapped endpoint), extracts a scope from the URL's arguments where the endpoint has one, forces a password change if the account is on a temporary password, and audits a refusal. Routes still do finer checks themselves: for example, a results route also asks whether this account may see this class ({{ file('blueprints/school/helpers.py') }}, _school_class_allowed).

An admin endpoint with no entry in the map is only guarded by admin.access, which nearly every staff account holds. When you add an admin route, add it to the map. A contract test helps, but it cannot know which permission you meant.

What the School workspace covers

The School workspace is the largest part of the product; each area has its own page.

  • Sessions, classes, subjects, students, enrolment, promotion, assignments, projects, tests, practice, examinations, exam timetables, attendance, results and report cards: {{ page('academics', 'Academics') }}.
  • Fees, payments and receipts: {{ page('finance', 'Finance and receipts') }}. Library books and loans: {{ file('blueprints/library/routes.py') }}.

The new-school setup checklist

The School workspace home page ({{ route('admin_school_home') }}) shows a grouped, ordered checklist of everything a new school puts in order: its profile and look, the academic session, classes and subjects (and which classes take which), staff, students and parents, fee items and billing, online payments, the receipt signature, the report card settings, attendance, the exam timetable, email/SMS and the library ({{ file('blueprints/school/onboarding.py') }}). The essentials are marked apart from the optional extras. Each step's state is read live from a plain count of the school's own active rows — never a stored flag — so it can never disagree with what the school has actually done. A school may dismiss it early ({{ route('admin_school_onboarding_dismiss') }}, one {{ table('school_settings') }} row) and bring it back ({{ route('admin_school_onboarding_show') }}); it disappears by itself once every step is done.

The Entrance workspace is described in {{ page('entrance', 'Entrance examinations') }}.

Top of the Class

On the Overview's second row, beside the People cards (Students and Parents), the same page shows the highest-scoring student in each class for the current term: one row per class, its top student's overall percentage and letter grade, reusing class_term_stats ({{ file('blueprints/school/report_card_data.py') }}) - the same aggregate the report-card pages already compute, never a separate calculation of its own. Which classes appear is scoped exactly like every other class-facing page: _school_class_allowed ({{ file('blueprints/school/helpers.py') }}) shows every active class to a School Admin and only a scoped teacher's own classes to them. A class with nobody ready yet (an official term result needs every one of a student's subjects released) shows as waiting rather than being silently left out.

Archiving: leavers keep their record

A student who leaves is archived, not deleted. Archiving sets the student's active flag to 0 and records archived_at, the administrator who archived them, and the reason. Because every current list, count and sign-in already filters on active, the archived student drops out of them all at once, and the same checks stop their login. Their open presence sessions are ended in the same step.

The archive and restore logic is one helper, {{ file('blueprints/school/helpers.py') }} (archive_students), used by the class register, the student page and the history import. Already archived students are left as they are, so the first archive's date and reason are never overwritten. A School Admin can restore a student; the archived list and each archived record are for the School Admin only ({{ route('admin_school_students_archived') }}).

Parents are deliberately not cut off: the parent dashboard, the child's record and the fee account include archived children, so the family keeps its history. Only the child's own sign-in is revoked.

Bulk imports: three steps

The bulk import page ({{ route('admin_school_students_import') }}) offers three steps, shown one at a time. Each step posts to its own route and has its own template, and each writes an audit entry that names who ran it, the file name and the counts.

Each step takes a CSV or an Excel workbook (.xlsx), read by {{ file('core/tabular.py') }} into the same rows, so every check after it is the same. A workbook is somebody else's file and is read defensively: recognised by its contents (a zip holding a workbook), never its name; macro-enabled (.xlsm, a vbaProject) and password-protected files refused; the archive checked for size and compression ratio before it is opened (no zip bombs); its XML parsed through defusedxml (without defusedxml installed, Excel files are refused); formulas never evaluated (data_only: the value Excel saved); the first sheet only, bounded in rows and columns.

What's new, the setup checklist and browser caching

Release notes are announced from one file, {{ file('release_notes.json') }}, read by {{ file('core/release_notes.py') }}. Each item has an audience: staff, family or student. Staff items keep a link to the matching guide page; family and student items are complete on their own and never carry one, because families cannot open the staff guide.

  • Administrators see the staff items in the panel at the bottom right of every admin page ({{ file('blueprints/admin_dock.py') }}, {{ file('templates/_admin_dock.html') }}).
  • Students and parents see their own items on their dashboard ({{ file('blueprints/family_notices.py') }}, {{ file('templates/_family_notice.html') }}). A student never receives a parent's item, and the reverse.
  • The database is not asked for any of this on an ordinary page. The server sends the notes for the audience, and the browser decides what to show, from the version it last saw for that account (stored in local storage). Closing a notice is a browser setting, so it does not reach another device.
  • The setup checklist is the one thing read from the database on request: {{ route('admin_school_onboarding_card') }} answers with the school's progress. The browser keeps that answer for fifteen minutes before asking again. Closing the checklist is a real change and is saved for the whole school.
  • The sign-in footer shows the version the platform is running, read from the newest release in {{ file('release_notes.json') }} by {{ file('blueprints/platform_version.py') }} and shown by {{ file('templates/login.html') }}. It needs no database read, and it always matches What's new, because both come from the same file.

Staff records and leave

{{ route('admin_hr_staff') }} ({{ file('blueprints/school/hr.py') }}) lists the school's staff records; the rules are {{ file('core/hr.py') }} and the tables {{ table('hr_staff') }} (one per person, optionally linked to an account by admin_id, unique), {{ table('hr_qualifications') }}, {{ table('hr_documents') }}, {{ table('hr_career_events') }}, {{ table('hr_leave_types') }}, {{ table('hr_leave_allowances') }} and {{ table('hr_leave_requests') }}. Permissions: {{ perm('hr.view') }}, {{ perm('hr.manage') }} and {{ perm('hr.leave.approve') }}; the HR Officer preset has all three, and the Head Teacher preset has {{ perm('hr.leave.approve') }}.

  • A record is read from a form by clean_record: known fields only, each cut to its length, choices from lists, dates real and in range. {{ route('admin_hr_person') }} sends no-store and logs hr_record_viewed; changes log the names of the fields changed (hr_record_updated), never their values. HR notes are never shown on {{ route('admin_my_leave') }}.
  • Documents must begin like what their extension says (PDF, Word, JPG, PNG; check_document) and are stored under a random name in uploads/hr/, a folder {{ file('core/upload_access.py') }} never opens. Only {{ route('admin_hr_document') }} serves them, to {{ perm('hr.view') }}, with nosniff and no-store, and logs hr_document_opened.
  • Career entries have no edit or delete route: {{ route('admin_hr_career_strike') }} marks one struck out, with who, when and why.
  • Leave is counted in working days (Monday to Friday) against the year of its first day. A request is refused if it overlaps a waiting or approved one, or takes more than is left less what is waiting; {{ route('admin_hr_leave_decide') }} locks the row, re-checks the balance and refuses anyone deciding leave on their own record. Requests notify every account with {{ perm('hr.leave.approve') }}; decisions notify the person. The menu badge is cached as hr-menu and dropped on any write.

Payroll

{{ route('admin_payroll') }} ({{ file('blueprints/school/payroll.py') }}); the rules are {{ file('core/payroll.py') }}, the tables {{ table('payroll_settings') }}, {{ table('payroll_items') }}, {{ table('payroll_staff') }}, {{ table('payroll_staff_items') }}, {{ table('payroll_runs') }}, {{ table('payroll_payslips') }} and {{ table('payroll_adjustments') }}. Permissions: {{ perm('payroll.view') }}, {{ perm('payroll.manage') }} and {{ perm('payroll.approve') }} (Payroll Officer: view and manage; approving is the School Admin's, and PRESET_ROLE_NEVER in {{ file('core/security.py') }} keeps payroll off the Finance Manager role in every school).

  • Money is integer kobo; percentages are basis points rounded half up (pct). PAYE is annualised: taxable pay x 12 less pension, NHF and rent relief, through the school's bands, / 12. The defaults are the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 bands and the PRA 2014 rates; each run stores the settings it used (settings_json) and each payslip its own lines (lines_json).
  • Paying through Paystack: {{ route('admin_payroll_paystack') }} ({{ file('blueprints/school/payroll_paystack.py') }}, the Paystack calls in {{ file('core/payroll_transfers.py') }}) is the school's own School Admin's alone (never a platform operator's). Each payslip keeps its own transfer (transfer_status, transfer_reference): marked pending and committed under a lock on the run before anything is sent, never sent again while pending or paid, settled only by /transfer/verify (from the signed webhook or Check transfers). Only the account on the approved payslip is paid (its bank and last four digits), after /bank/resolve; the password is asked again, and only wrong ones count towards its limit. A run with any transfer pending or paid cannot be reopened.
  • Four eyes: {{ route('admin_payroll_run_approve') }} refuses the run's prepared_by, which every change (work out again, a one-off line, reopening) resets to whoever made it. Nobody edits their own pay or payslip. Approved runs refuse changes; paid runs are final.
  • Secrets: account numbers, RSA PINs and tax IDs are Fernet-encrypted under an HKDF key per school from BRIGHTSTARS_DELIVERY_KEY, and included in {{ file('core/secrets_rotation.py') }}. They are decrypted only by {{ route('admin_payroll_run_bank') }} and {{ route('admin_payroll_run_returns') }}, for an approved run, for payroll.manage or payroll.approve; both are logged and CSV-defused. Pages with salaries are no-store; opening a run or payslip is logged, and logs name what changed, never amounts or numbers.
  • {{ route('admin_payroll_payslip') }} serves payroll.view, or the person themselves once the run is approved; {{ route('admin_my_payslips') }} lists their own. The run page shows each net against the last approved or paid month, so an unagreed rise stands out to the approver.
  • Load: the newer areas are held to query budgets by tests/verification/write_paths_query_budget.py (a page's queries do not grow with the number of staff). Balances, unpaid leave and payslips are read in bulk; approvers come from admins_holding() ({{ file('core/security.py') }}) in one query; logged pages are drawn before the log commits; the menu counts (hr-menu, staff-clock-menu, payroll-menu) are dropped only by a change in their own area, and a student's or candidate's write drops no staff cache at all. {{ file('core/short_cache.py') }} holds entries school by school, at most 50,000, dropping expired entries and then the least recently served schools, never everything at once.

Staff attendance

{{ route('admin_clock') }} ({{ file('blueprints/school/staff_attendance.py') }}) is where staff clock in and out; {{ route('admin_staff_attendance') }} is the register. The rules are {{ file('core/staff_attendance.py') }}; the tables {{ table('staff_attendance_settings') }} (one row), {{ table('staff_attendance_records') }} (one per staff record and day, unique), {{ table('staff_attendance_devices') }}, {{ table('staff_attendance_corrections') }} and {{ table('staff_attendance_closed_days') }}. Permissions: {{ perm('staff_attendance.view') }} and {{ perm('staff_attendance.manage') }}. The school's day is kept in its own time zone, tenants.timezone, chosen by a platform admin from {{ file('control_plane/timezones.py') }} when the school is created or on its page ({{ route('platform_school_timezone') }}); Africa/Lagos, which is all of Nigeria, unless changed.

  • The code is an HMAC of the 30-second window under code_secret (QR text and six digits); the current and two earlier windows are accepted. {{ route('staff_attendance_display') }} and {{ route('staff_attendance_display_code') }} need no sign-in and answer only for the token whose hash is display_token_hash; a new link also renews code_secret.
  • The location must be within radius_m and at least as sure as max_accuracy_m; only the distance and accuracy are stored. The clock and settings pages send Permissions-Policy: geolocation=(self).
  • The phone: a random tag in an HttpOnly cookie, its SHA-256 in {{ table('staff_attendance_devices') }}; registered on the first successful clock-in, refused on any other until {{ route('admin_staff_attendance_device_reset') }}. {{ route('admin_clock_punch') }} allows 12 tries per account per ten minutes.
  • A day's status (day_status): in or late from the record; then approved leave; a closed day; not a work day; not counted before started_on, before employment or after leaving; otherwise absent (or not in yet today). Hand-marking ({{ route('admin_staff_attendance_mark') }}) needs a note and never overwrites a phone time; corrections are decided by {{ route('admin_staff_attendance_correction_decide') }}, never by the person who asked.

ID cards

{{ route('admin_id_cards') }} ({{ file('blueprints/school/id_cards.py') }}) prints student and staff cards from the school's design; {{ route('admin_id_card_designer') }} edits it. The rules are {{ file('core/id_cards.py') }}; the tables {{ table('id_card_designs') }} (one design per kind, with a lock) and {{ table('id_cards') }} (each card issued: a random token, a serial, active or cancelled). Permissions: {{ perm('id_cards.print') }} and {{ perm('id_cards.manage') }}; teaching staff print only for the class (or subdivision) they are class teacher of, and never staff cards. Every design studio (STUDIO_ENDPOINTS in {{ file('core/security.py') }}) opens for the School Admin and a head teacher, and for anyone else only with the studio's permission given personally (may_use_studios), never through a role alone.

  • The design is positioned boxes in millimetres on a CR80 card (design version 2: text and details with fonts, weights, spacing and alignment; shapes with fills, gradients and outlines; the photograph, logo, the school's own images ({{ table('id_card_assets') }}, uploaded to uploads/idcards/) and signatures with a fit, a clip shape and a frame; the QR code and a Code 128 barcode; each with rotation, opacity and a shadow). clean_layout rebuilds anything saved from known keys only: element types, fonts, shapes, clips and fields from allow-lists, colours #rrggbb or the school's brand/accent, every number clamped, text cut and stripped of control characters, images only the school's own, at most 60 items a side. One renderer, {{ file('static/id-card-render.js') }}, draws the designer's canvas and the print sheet with createElement, textContent and style properties only. Each save is kept in {{ table('id_card_design_versions') }} (the newest 20 a kind); {{ route('admin_id_card_design_version') }} brings one back into the designer. A locked design refuses saves and resets (409), and an image it uses cannot be removed ({{ route('admin_id_card_asset_delete') }}).
  • Printing ({{ route('admin_id_cards_print') }}) issues a card to anyone without an active one, and is refused whole if any person is outside the account's reach (another class, a leaver, a removed account). The sheet is no-store and logged (id_cards_printed).
  • The QR code is drawn on the server (reportlab's encoder, as an SVG path) and holds only /id/<token>. {{ route('id_card_verify') }} and {{ route('id_card_photo') }} need no sign-in, answer only for a real token, show only the school, name, photograph and class or role, and send no-store, noindex and no referrer. The photograph is served only while the card is active and the person current.

A card's QR code is made once at its own size, each row's dark modules one bar.
  • Design studios ({{ route('admin_design_studios') }}, {{ file('blueprints/school/design_studios.py') }}) is the one menu entry for both studios: it shows each studio only to someone who may open it.

The school store

Settings, and the activity and notice logs

{{ route('admin_settings') }} ({{ file('blueprints/school/settings.py') }}) is one list of every setting, each shown only to whoever may open it. Every settings page is drawn inside the same frame by {{ file('templates/admin_base.html') }} (the list on the left, the page beside it) and loads in place; staff and roles open in pop-ups. The activity log ({{ route('admin_audit_logs') }}) and the notice log ({{ route('admin_audit_notices') }}) are two tabs, two addresses, so opening one never reads the other; both page fifty records at a time and search on the server. A student's notice log ({{ route('admin_notification_log') }}) opens in a pop-up from their record.

Loading. A view that loads in place ({{ file('static/inplace.js') }}) dims its page body under a spinner in the school's accent while the next one is fetched. Every other load - the side menu into another section, a form sent as a whole page, a workspace opened from the door - shows the same thing through {{ file('static/page-loading.js') }}, which {{ file('templates/admin_base.html') }} and {{ file('templates/admin_workspace_home.html') }} load (and the student and parent portals and the sign-in pages, {{ file('templates/auth_base.html') }}, too). It acts only on a click or form that nothing else has handled, leaves addresses that answer with a file alone (.../pdf, /export/..., CSV templates, attachments, uploads), and clears itself after fifteen seconds if the page has not gone, so a "leave this page?" answered Stay never leaves it dimmed. The installed app has no browser bar of its own, so without this it sat still until the next page appeared.

A whole-page load also bridges the moment one page gives way to the next. As a page is left, its content gives way to the outline of a page ({{ file('templates/includes/_skeleton.html') }}: a title line, a figure strip and two panels of rows, softly pulsing) under the spinner, and the loader notes the time in sessionStorage. The next page reads it first thing in its <head> ({{ file('templates/includes/_pwa_head.html') }}, inline, needing no other file) and is drawn as the same outline from its first paint; its content is shown only once it has loaded (the load event, then document.fonts.ready, then two frames, so it has been painted in full), and fades in. So the outline is on screen from the press until the next page is complete, with no blank, frozen or half-drawn screen between. The outline sits beside the page body in a .bs-stage, so a view loaded in place (which keeps its own dim-and-spinner) never removes it. That moment is kept short too: every url_for('static', ...) names a file by its modification time alone (a hand-written v= is dropped, {{ file('core/speed.py') }}), so two pages share one cached copy of a stylesheet instead of each fetching its own; fonts are no longer fetched twice (the preload and fonts.css now name the same address); admin.css no longer @imports the fonts' stylesheet (a round trip before the first paint); and the workspace chooser prefetches the staff frame's styles and scripts while it is read, so the workspace is drawn the moment it arrives. While a workspace is on its way, the chooser takes the workspace's own frame (a stand-in for its menu rail on the left, the outline where its content goes, and no chooser role line or Sign out), so the wait looks like the workspace arriving rather than the chooser still waiting. The chooser answers one press only: a small script on {{ file('templates/admin_workspace_home.html') }} locks both doors on the first press (the chosen button shows a spinner in place of its words, the other fades) and nothing unlocks them - not the loader giving up, not Back from the browser's memory - until the page is loaded again.

Scripts that send forms read form.getAttribute('method'), 'action' and 'target', never form.method and the like: a field named method (the store's Cash / Bank transfer / POS radio buttons) or target replaces the property, and {{ file('static/inplace.js') }} once failed on it, leaving Record a payment spinning with nothing sent. {{ file('tests/current/test_form_attributes_contract.py') }} keeps it that way.

The staff guide

/admin/guide ({{ file('blueprints/school/guide.py') }}) is a plain-language, hand-written user guide for the people who use the portal — not this documentation, which is for platform operators and generated from the code. It carries no permission of its own: an endpoint left out of ADMIN_ENDPOINT_PERMISSIONS falls back to admin.access, which nearly every staff account holds, so any signed-in admin can read the whole thing in either workspace without switching. Seventeen pages, in six groups that follow the order of a school year (getting started, setting up the school, students and families, academics, money, store and library, and the entrance workspace), with screenshots of the screens they describe ({{ file('templates/school_guide/_macros.html') }}'s shot, pictures in static/guide/ taken from a demonstration school), with a search box (a plain substring match over the rendered text of every page, no code introspection) and an on-this-page outline built from its own headings. Its own contract test, {{ file('tests/current/test_guide_contract.py') }}, checks every link and slug the same way {{ file('tests/current/test_docs_contract.py') }} checks this documentation.

Accounts other people get

Students, parents and entrance candidates are created by staff, never self-registered. Each receives a generated one-time password and must change it at first sign-in (students and parents; candidates use the credentials printed for them). Student sign-in names come from the admission number; numbers themselves come from the school's numbering rules.
