Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023 makes the school responsible for its families' information: what it may do with it, and a family's right to a copy of it or to have it erased. This page covers what families allow (consents), their requests, a full copy of the school's own data, and the API that lets the school's own systems read its data.

What families allow

For each child, a parent decides three things in their portal, under Privacy & my data. All three are allowed until a parent says no, and a parent can change their mind at any time:

  • Photos in publicity: whether the school may use the child's photograph on its website, social media, brochures and banners. ID cards and report cards use the photograph regardless; this is about publicity. Check the list (below) before publishing a picture: some families will have said no.
  • Optional messages: birthday greetings and any other optional text or email. Messages about fees, results, attendance and safety always go.
  • Exam registration: whether the child's details and photograph may be shared with WAEC, NECO or the BECE examining body; see Exam registration below.

A parent is also asked, on their home page, to read the privacy notice and confirm it; they are asked again whenever it changes.

A family that does not use the portal

Open the student's page and its Consents tab. It shows what is in force for each purpose and how it was decided. Under Record from a signed form choose the purpose and what the family decided, and say what the school holds (for example Signed by the mother, 12 September); keep the form. Every decision, from a parent or from a form, stays in the history with who recorded it and when. This needs the right to answer data requests (the head teacher, the academic administrator and the records officers have it; the School Admin always).

Whose photos may be used

Privacy & data requests (Settings, Your school) counts, for each purpose, how many students it is allowed for. Download, child by child gives a spreadsheet of every student with each answer, marked (default) where nobody has decided yet: check it before a photograph goes on the website or a banner.

A family's requests

From Privacy & my data a parent can ask for a copy of the data held about a child, or for it to be erased. The School Admin is told, and the request waits on Privacy & data requests with the date it must be answered by: thirty days, as the law requires. Check that it really comes from the child's parent before you approve it.

  • A copy: Approve and prepare the copy builds a ZIP: the child's record and admission form, classes, attendance, released results, fees, payments, documents issued, consents, and the parent's own messages and account details. The parent downloads it from their portal for seven days. Nothing about another parent is included, and no password ever is.
  • Erasure is the School Admin's alone, confirmed by typing the child's admission number. The child's name becomes a placeholder (Former student #42), and their contact, health and religious details, photograph, sign-in, messages, alerts, admission form and entrance record are removed. Results, attendance, fees and payments stay, under the placeholder name, because the school must keep them; a transcript or testimonial can no longer be issued, and any issued before are marked revoked. A parent with no other child at the school is anonymised too and can no longer sign in. It cannot be undone.
  • Declining needs a reason, which the family sees.

Exam registration

On Exam registration every candidate is included unless their family has said no to sharing: such a candidate shows Missing: consent to share and is left out of both downloads (the candidates' spreadsheet and the photographs ZIP). If the family changes its mind, the parent allows it again in their portal, or, under Consent to share, tick the candidate, say what the school holds (Signed form collected 14 October), and record it; they are included at once.

Export all the school's data

Export all the school's data (Settings, Your school; the School Admin's alone) prepares one ZIP: a spreadsheet for every kind of record the school keeps, from students, parents and staff to results, attendance, fees, payments and messages, and every uploaded file (photographs, signatures, attachments), with a README that says what each is. Use it as a backup, or to take the school's data to another system. It is prepared in the background (a large school takes a few minutes; refresh the page), kept for seven days, then removed.

Left out on purpose: passwords and other sign-in secrets, and the school's stored keys for email, SMS and Paystack. The file holds every family's personal data: keep it as carefully as the school's paper records.

The API, for the school's own systems

API & webhooks (Settings, Your school; the School Admin's alone) lets the school's own developers, or a partner it hires (an accounting system, the school website), read its data automatically.

  • A key: name it after who will use it and tick only the areas it needs (students, parents, classes, attendance, released results, fees, payments). The key is shown once: copy it and hand it over safely. A key can only read; nothing can be changed through it. Revoke stops it at once.
  • Webhooks: an address on the school's (or partner's) system that is told the moment something happens: a payment recorded or voided, a student registered or archived, a report card ready. Each notice carries ids, never names or contact details, and is signed with a secret shown once when the address is added. Send a test checks it works; the latest deliveries show under each address, and a failed one can be sent again. An address that keeps failing is switched off, with the reason shown.
  • Documentation for developers opens a page on the school's own address explaining everything a developer needs, including how to check a notice's signature.

Troubleshooting

A parent says they cannot find Privacy & my data.
It is in the parent portal's menu, under The school. On a phone, open the menu (it is not in the bottom bar).
A child's photograph shows "Not allowed" but the family now agrees.
A parent said no earlier. Ask them to allow it again in their portal, or record their signed form on the student's Consents tab.
A family stopped getting birthday texts.
A parent withdrew Optional messages. Only they (or a new signed form) can turn it back on.
A candidate is missing from the exam registration download.
Their family said no to sharing. If they change their mind, record the signed form under Consent to share on Exam registration, or the parent allows it again in their portal.
I cannot erase a child's data.
Only the School Admin can, and the admission number must be typed exactly as shown. A head teacher can see and decline requests, and approve copies.
We erased a child by mistake.
It cannot be undone. The results and fees are still there under the placeholder name; the details can be entered again on the student's record if the family agrees.
The family's copy says it has expired.
Copies are kept for seven days. Ask the parent to request again, and approve it.
The export says it could not be prepared.
Press Prepare an export again. If it fails a second time, tell support: the reason is recorded.
I lost an API key or a webhook secret.
They are never shown again. Revoke the key and make a new one; remove the webhook address and add it again.
The developer gets "401" or "403".
401: the key is wrong or revoked. 403: the key was not given that area; make a new key with it ticked.
A webhook address was switched off.
It failed too many times in a row; the reason shows under it. Once the receiving system is fixed, press Switch on, then Send a test.
Adding a webhook address says it is not allowed.
It must start with https://, and it must be on the public internet: an address inside the school's own network cannot be used.
