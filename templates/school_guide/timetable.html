Build the exam or test timetable as a draft, check it, then release it all at once.

Building the draft

Under Exam Timetable, add one entry per class, subject, date and time, with an optional venue. Entries can be edited or deleted freely while they are a draft — nothing outside staff sees them yet.

Releasing

Releasing turns every not-yet-released entry for the session and term into a released one, in one action, and — in the background, so you are not left waiting — notifies every currently enrolled student of the classes it covers, and their parents: an in-app alert each, plus an email and a WhatsApp message to the guardian contact. Releasing again releases nothing more, and tells you so.

Releasing is its own permission, separate from adding or editing entries, so a school can decide exactly who is trusted to notify everyone at once.

What students and parents see

A student sees only the released entries for their own class; a linked parent sees the same for their child. Either can download it as a PDF or print it directly from the page.
