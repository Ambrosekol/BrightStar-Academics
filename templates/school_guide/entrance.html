The Entrance workspace runs the exam a candidate sits before they are ever a student here — kept deliberately apart from the School workspace (see Welcome).

Question banks and exam configuration

Question Banks hold the questions themselves; Exam Configuration decides which bank is the live paper for each entry class and subject, for the current academic period. A bank being used by a live exam can be locked to stop it changing mid-way.

Candidates

Candidates registers who is sitting the exam and prints or resets their sign-in credentials. A candidate signs in with those credentials, sits the paper once, and their attempt is recorded automatically as they go.

Results and rankings

Results & Analytics shows every candidate's outcome; Rankings orders them. A candidate's own result can be shared as an image carrying the school's own branding, nothing of another school's.

Entrance practice

Practice Tests lets anyone, with no registration, try a practice paper for the class they are aiming for — from either the last session's own entrance exam, or a custom bank the school sets up for practice. The current session's own live bank is never offered for practice, so it cannot reveal an exam that has not been sat yet.

From candidate to student

Once a candidate has sat at least one paper, they move onto the {{ page('admissions', 'admissions waitlist') }} — where an admissions officer decides to admit them into a real, enrolled student account, or decline them.
