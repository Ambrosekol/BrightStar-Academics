Problems reported by a school's own people. To see what they see, enter the school from its console page ({{ route('platform_school_enter') }}): you arrive as the School Admin, through a reserved account, and everything you do is recorded on both sides. For "a teacher cannot see X", remember you see everything; check the teacher's own roles and duties instead (below).

Changes to staff, roles and permissions show at once on the server that saved them and within BRIGHTSTARS_ADMIN_CACHE_SECONDS (15) elsewhere; menu counts within BRIGHTSTARS_BADGE_CACHE_SECONDS (10). If something "did not save", wait a few seconds and reload before assuming it failed.

Staff access

"This action is not available to you"

You see
This action is not available to you · Your staff role does not currently include access to this area.
Why

Every page needs a permission (mapped in ADMIN_ENDPOINT_PERMISSIONS, {{ file('core/security.py') }}); the person's roles do not include it. Some areas are kept for the School Admin alone and can never be given to a role: Staff and Staff roles, Email & SMS, Online payments, School profile, Academic sessions, Promotion, Class subdivisions.

Fix
  1. Find which permission: the permissions reference ({{ page('reference-permissions', 'Permissions and roles') }}) lists what each one opens.
  2. The School Admin gives the person a role that has it (Settings › Staff › the person › roles), or adds it to their role (Settings › Staff roles). An override on one person is for exceptions.
  3. If the area is School Admin only, the School Admin does it themself.
Check
After a reload the page opens for them.

A menu item is missing for someone

You see
A teacher has no Class transfers, a cashier no Student accounts, someone no Settings entries.
Why

Each menu item shows only to those who may open it (one list in {{ tpl('admin_base.html') }}). Examples: Class transfers is for JSS/SSS class teachers, head teachers and the School Admin; Student accounts needs View all finance; Teachers needs Assign teaching duties.

Fix

Give the role the matching permission, or the matching teaching duty (below).

Check
The item appears after a reload.

A teacher sees every class, or not the class they teach

You see
A new teacher sees every class's students; or a teacher cannot find their own class.
Why

Teaching duties ({{ table('teaching_duties') }}) decide where a teacher works. A teacher with no duties yet reaches every class; once they have duties, those are all they see. A class-teacher duty can be for a whole class or for one subdivision (JSS 1A); a subject duty is for a subject in a class (and, in SSS, a department).

Fix

Set their duties on the staff form or the Teachers page ({{ route('admin_school_teaching') }}). For a class split into subdivisions, tick the subdivision they are class teacher of.

Check
Their student list shows exactly their students.

"Releasing results is for the class teacher of this class"

You see
A subject teacher, or a class teacher of another class, cannot release results.
Why

Results go subject teacher → class teacher. Only the class teacher of that class (or of that subdivision, for its students), a head teacher, or the School Admin releases them ({{ file('blueprints/school/results_records.py') }}).

Fix

The subject teacher sends marks on (Send to class teacher); the class teacher releases. Make sure the class has a class teacher (Teachers page shows "Not assigned" otherwise).

Check
The class teacher sees the results as ready to release.

Classes, subdivisions and transfers

"Class subdivisions are locked. Unlock them first to make changes."

You see
Class subdivisions are locked. Unlock them first to make changes.
Why

The School Admin locked the set-up so it is not changed by accident ({{ route('admin_school_subdivisions') }}).

Fix

The School Admin presses Unlock to make changes, edits, and locks again. Moving a single student does not need unlocking: use Class transfers.

Check
The change saves.

Students "not placed" in a subdivision

You see
The Class subdivisions page shows "N not placed" for a class.
Why

Students are placed in turn into the subdivision with the fewest students ({{ file('core/subdivisions.py') }}). In SSS a subdivision with a department only takes students of that department; a student whose department has no subdivision (Art students in a class whose subdivisions are all Science) cannot be placed.

Fix

Add a subdivision for that department, or one with no department, and save the class: saving places everyone not yet placed. Or correct the student's department.

Check
The count of students not placed is 0.

A class transfer is waiting and nobody can approve it

You see
A transfer stays in Waiting on others, listed as waiting for "the School Admin or a head teacher".
Why

The class teacher of the subdivision the student would join approves it ({{ file('blueprints/school/transfers.py') }}). Nobody approves their own request. If that subdivision has no class teacher, or it is a department change in a class without subdivisions (the same teacher would ask and approve), only the School Admin or a head teacher can.

Fix

The School Admin or a head teacher opens Class transfers ({{ route('admin_school_transfers') }}) and approves or declines it. Give the subdivision a class teacher so future transfers go to them.

Check
The student appears in the receiving teacher's list.

"The subdivision this student would join has been removed, so the transfer cannot be made."

You see
Approving a transfer fails with this message (or "The student is no longer in that class this session").
Why

Something changed since the request: the subdivision was removed, or the student changed class.

Fix

Decline it, and ask again if still needed.

Check
The transfer moves to History as declined.

Students and sessions

"There is no active academic session to enrol students into."

You see
Registering or importing students, billing, the store or a timetable says there is no (current) session.
Why

Almost everything belongs to a session; one session must be current.

Fix

The School Admin creates the session and marks it current: Settings › Academic sessions ({{ route('admin_school_sessions') }}). "Set another session as current before archiving this one" and "Reactivate this session before making it current" are the same rule seen from the other side.

Check
The Overview shows the session's name.

"This student is archived. Restore them from Archived students instead."

You see
An archived student cannot be edited, enrolled or sign in.
Why

Archiving keeps the record and takes the student out of everything current. Their parents still see them, under Children who have left the school.

Fix

The School Admin restores them from Archived students ({{ route('admin_school_students_archived') }}); their sign-in works again.

Check
The student is back on their class register.

Results and report cards

"That report card is not ready…" / a parent cannot see a report card

You see
That report card is not ready: every one of the student's results for the term has to be released first.
Why

A card is worked out from released results and appears only when every result of the student for that term is released ({{ file('blueprints/school/report_card_data.py') }}). One result still waiting holds the whole card back.

Fix

On Report cards ({{ route('admin_school_report_cards') }}) the class shows each card as ready, or waiting with how many results. Find the waiting subjects on Results ({{ route('admin_school_results') }}): recorded marks need sending to the class teacher, then releasing.

Check
The card shows as ready; parents are told by email, SMS and in-app.

"This result must be … before it can be …"

You see
This result must be approved before it can be released. (or similar) · There are no approved results to release for this term. · Approved or released results cannot be edited here.
Why

Results move in order: recorded → sent to the class teacher (approved) → released. A released result is part of the record and is not edited in place.

Fix

Take the step that comes first. Before release, a class teacher can send a wrong mark back to its subject teacher to correct. After release, the result is part of the record: it is changed only through the authorised correction process, not edited in place.

Check
The result reaches released.

Students or parents cannot see released results

You see
A teacher says results are done, but the student's portal shows nothing.
Why

Students and parents only ever see released results. Approved results wait for the class teacher to release them, or for the school's release date (set on Results), when every approved result is released at once. In SSS, a student sees only subjects their department takes.

Fix

Check the results' state on Results: release them, or wait for the release date. Check the student's department and the subject's departments.

Check
The results show in the student's portal.

The student and parent portals

A result shows "Awaiting marking" and will not move on

You see
"Awaiting marking" in a student's results; "This result is waiting for its theory answers to be marked, so it cannot move on yet."; or a report card that is not ready.
Why

The test or examination has theory questions, and at least one of the student's written answers has no mark yet. Until it does the result has no score ({{ file('core/theory_marking.py') }}).

Fix
  1. The subject teacher opens To mark ({{ route('admin_school_marking') }}), the paper, and the student, and gives every written answer a mark.
  2. The teacher does not see the paper or the student? They need the edit permission for that kind of paper ({{ perm('school.tests.edit') }} or {{ perm('school.examinations.edit') }}) and a teaching duty for that subject in that class (in SSS, that student's department).
Check
The result shows a score and can be sent to the class teacher.

"its marks are locked" / the split "can no longer change"

You see
"This result has been released, so its marks are locked." or "Results of this paper have been released, so how its marks are shared can no longer change."
Why

A released result is part of the student's record and is never re-scored by marking; once any result of a paper is released, its objective/theory split is fixed for everyone.

Fix

Correct a released result through the result correction process. Decide the split before releasing.

Check
The result reads correctly in the student's results.

A payroll cannot be approved, or a payslip is wrong

You see
"You prepared or last changed this payroll, so someone else approves it"; "A payslip takes more than it pays"; or PAYE, pension or NHF that the accountant disagrees with.
Why

Four eyes: whoever prepared, worked out again, added a line to or reopened a run ({{ table('payroll_runs') }}, prepared_by) cannot approve it. A negative net pay is refused. Statutory amounts come from the school's own payroll settings ({{ table('payroll_settings') }}), copied into each run.

Fix
  1. Ask another person with {{ perm('payroll.approve') }} (the bursar, or the School Admin) to approve it.
  2. Reduce the one-off deductions on that payslip, or correct the person's pay, then Work out again.
  3. Have the accountant correct Payroll, Settings; then Work out again on a draft. Approved months keep the settings they were approved with; reopen one only if it is not paid.
Check
The month shows Approved, and staff see the payslip on My payslips.

A member of staff cannot clock in

You see
"That code is not the one on the school's screen now"; "only sure of where it is to within N m"; "N m from the school"; "This is not the phone you clock in with"; or no Clock in in their menu.
Why

The code changes every 30 seconds and is accepted for about a minute. Indoors, phones often cannot locate themselves well. Each person clocks in from one phone ({{ table('staff_attendance_devices') }}). Clock in shows only to someone with a staff record linked to their account, while attendance is on.

Fix
  1. Scan the screen again just before pressing Clock in; check the screen is online.
  2. Turn on precise location and step outside or near a window; for a large compound, raise the allowed distance or the accuracy in Settings.
  3. Changed phones: Settings, Clock-in phones, Reset.
  4. Meanwhile, mark them in by hand on the register, with a note.
Check
Their time appears on the register as "phone".

A member of staff cannot ask for leave, or leave cannot be approved

You see
"You have no staff record yet"; "only N are left"; a request nobody can approve; or "it is not one you may decide".
Why

My leave needs a staff record linked to the person's account ({{ table('hr_staff') }}, admin_id). Leave is refused when it takes more working days than are left for the year, counting requests still waiting. Nobody decides leave on their own record, so the HR Officer's own leave needs someone else with {{ perm('hr.leave.approve') }}.

Fix
  1. On Staff records, open (or add) their record and choose their account under Portal account; or press "Add records for them".
  2. Days carried over: on their record's Leave tab, change their allowance for the year.
  3. Give {{ perm('hr.leave.approve') }} to a second person (the Head Teacher preset has it), or the School Admin decides.
Check
The person sees My leave in their menu, and the request appears on Staff leave for someone else to decide.

ID cards print the wrong size, a card shows "Not valid", or the design will not save

You see
Printed cards are too small or too large; a card's QR code says "Not valid"; or "The design is locked. Unlock it to make changes."
Why

The sheet is drawn at real size (85.6 × 54 mm); a printer set to "fit to page" shrinks it. A card is not valid once it was cancelled or replaced, or when the person has left the school (archived) or their account was removed. A locked design ({{ table('id_card_designs') }}) refuses changes until unlocked.

Fix
  1. Print at 100% (scale "Actual size"), with no margins added by the printer.
  2. "Not valid" by mistake: check the person is still active and enrolled; a replaced card's old one stays cancelled, so print the new one.
  3. To change the design, someone with {{ perm('id_cards.manage') }} presses Unlock on the designer.
Check
A card measures like a bank card, and its QR code opens a page saying "Valid card".

The design studios

The ID card, report card and receipt studios ({{ page('school-portal', 'The school portal') }}, {{ page('finance', 'Finance and receipts') }}). A design is saved per school; report cards and receipts use it only once switched on. Every save, lock and switch is in the activity log.

A studio shows "Open this on a computer"

You see
Open this on a computer · Designing ID cards, report cards and receipts needs a mouse and a larger screen.
Why

A phone (recognised by its browser's description, phone_notice in {{ file('blueprints/school/design_studios.py') }}) is sent this page instead of a studio; on any other device, a window narrower than 1024 pixels shows the same message over the studio ({{ file('static/id-card-designer.css') }}). It is about a usable screen, not access.

Fix

Use a laptop or desktop, with the browser window wider than 1024 pixels (or zoomed out).

Check
The studio opens.

Design studios, or one studio, is not in someone's menu

You see
No Design studios under Management, Design & print; or the page shows only some of the three studios.
Why

Each studio needs its own permission: {{ perm('id_cards.manage') }}, {{ perm('report_cards.manage') }}, {{ perm('finance.manage') }}. The menu entry shows when the person holds at least one; the page shows only those they hold.

Fix

Give the person a role with the right permission (Records Officer, Report Card Officer, Finance Manager, Head Teacher).

Check
After a reload the entry and the studio appear.

Report cards or receipts still use the standard design

You see
The school designed and saved its own, but PDFs and pages look the standard way.
Why

A design is used only while switched on (Use this design, in_use on {{ table('report_card_designs') }} / {{ table('receipt_designs') }}), and always as last saved. Workers keep it for BRIGHTSTARS_DESIGN_CACHE_SECONDS (60): the worker that saved or switched shows it at once, others within that time.

Fix
  1. In the studio, check the switch says In use; press Save after the last change.
  2. Wait a minute, then reload.
Check
A new PDF carries the design.

The log says "The report card design could not be read; using the standard card" (or the receipt one)

You see
Cards or receipts fall back to the standard design; the error log has that line with a traceback.
Why

The stored design (layout_json) is not valid JSON, which only happens if it was changed outside the studio. Cards never fail because of it: they fall back to the standard design.

Fix

In the studio, bring back a version (Versions) or pick a template, and save. Or set in_use to 0 until it is fixed.

Check
The log line stops and the design is used again.

A design's image is blank on a parent's or student's report card

You see
A watermark, stamp or badge shows in the studio but is blank on the report card page.
Why

Those pictures come from {{ route('report_design_image') }}, which answers only someone signed in at that school, and only for an image the design in use shows (otherwise 404). The browser keeps each one for a week.

Fix

Check the design is switched on and saved with the image; check the image file still exists ({{ page('manual-files', 'Files') }}).

Check
The image loads at its address for a signed-in parent.

Attendance, the position in class or a personal detail is empty on a report card

You see
Days present, the position (3rd of 34), the date of birth or the guardian is blank.
Why

Attendance counts the days the class register was taken that term ({{ table('attendance_records') }}). The position ranks only students whose card is ready. Personal details come from the student's record; health details are never offered. A page carries only the details its design shows.

Fix

Take the register; release the class's results; fill in the student's record.

Check
The next card shows the figure.

"The … design is locked and uses this image. Unlock it first."

You see
Removing one of the school's design images is refused (409).
Why

The images ({{ table('id_card_assets') }}) are shared by all three studios, and a locked design keeps the images it uses (remove_design_image, {{ file('blueprints/school/id_cards.py') }}).

Fix

Unlock the design the message names, then remove the image.

Check
The image is removed and disappears from unlocked designs.

"My paper submitted itself", "it says I left the page", or full screen does not start

You see
A student's paper ended early; a student is warned they left the paper when they did not mean to; or the paper did not go full screen (an iPhone).
Why

Exam mode ({{ file('core/exam_integrity.py') }}) records leaving the page or full screen. A notification, switching apps, a power cut or a lost connection all count. With a leave limit set on the paper, going past it submits the paper on the server. iPhones do not allow a web page to go full screen; the paper still works and the record says so.

Fix
  1. Read the paper's Exam mode record tab: how often, and whether exam mode ended it.
  2. Ended unfairly (a power cut): the student's answers up to then were saved. Correct the result through the usual correction process, or set the paper again for that student.
  3. Too strict for your students' conditions: clear End the paper after… in the paper's Settings, or switch exam mode off for that paper.
Check
The record matches what the student describes.

A paper "sat on paper": students cannot see it, or a booklet is missing someone

You see
"This is sat on paper, so it is never opened to students online."; students cannot find a test; or the answer booklets leave a student out.
Why

A printed paper ({{ file('core/printed_papers.py') }}) is never opened online, by design. Booklets are printed for students who are active, enrolled in the paper's class for the paper's session, take the subject (in SSS, by department), and whom the printing teacher teaches.

Fix
  1. Meant to be on the computer? Change How students sit it in the paper's Settings (only possible before anyone has sat it).
  2. A student is missing from the booklets: check their enrolment for that session and class, their department (SSS), and the teacher's teaching duty.
  3. No photograph on a booklet: add the student's photo on their page; the booklet otherwise has a box to paste one in.
Check
The booklets list every student who sits the paper.

A parent cannot see their child

You see
"No students are linked to this account", or one child missing.
Why

A parent sees only children linked to their account by an active link. Admitting a candidate creates the parent and the link; a parent added by hand must be linked.

Fix

On the parent's record (Parents), link the child. A child who left is under Children who have left the school (closed until opened), not missing.

Check
The child appears on the parent's Overview.

A student cannot see a test or assignment

You see
A teacher set a test, but the student's portal does not show it.
Why

A student sees work for their own class in the current session, and in SSS only subjects their department takes. Work for a chosen few students reaches only those.

Fix

Check the student's class this session and department, and who the work was issued to.

Check
The work appears.

A brand colour is refused

You see
The school profile refuses a colour as too light.
Why

Both brand colours sit behind white text, so each must have a contrast of at least 4.5:1 with white (WCAG AA) ({{ file('core/theme.py') }}).

Fix

Choose a darker shade of the same colour; the form shows when it passes.

Check
The colour saves and the portal uses it.
