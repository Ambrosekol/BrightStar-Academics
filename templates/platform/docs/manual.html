Three ways to find your problem

  1. Type a few words of the message into the filter below: the list narrows as you type.
  2. Search everything with the box at the top of the page: it finds the exact problem (results marked Fix), plus pages, files, routes, tables and permissions.
  3. Read the list below, grouped by area, or go to {{ page('manual-messages', 'Every message, explained') }} for every message the portal can show.

When you do not know where to start

Five questions, in this order, find the area of almost any problem:

  1. Is the application running? curl -s https://ANY-ADDRESS/health answers {"status":"ok"}. No answer: {{ page('manual-startup', "It won't start, or a deploy failed") }} or {{ page('manual-addresses', 'Addresses, domains and proxies') }}.
  2. Is it every school, or one? Every school: the server, the registry or a deploy. One school: its address, its database, or it is suspended.
  3. Is it everyone in the school, or one person? One person: their sign-in, their role and duties, their device.
  4. What does the screen say, exactly? Search those words here.
  5. What does the log say at that moment? journalctl -u beacon --since "15 min ago" (or wherever your server writes gunicorn's output). The last Error line is usually the cause.
