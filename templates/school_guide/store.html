The school store sells what parents buy from the school — uniforms, books, sportswear, bags — and keeps track of everything until it is handed over. Store money is kept apart from school fees: a store purchase never shows as a fee payment waiting to be applied.

The menu has a Store group with three pages: Inventory, Purchase claims and Record a payment. You see them if your role includes View the store (Inventory and Purchase claims) or Run the store (everything, including adding items, recording payments and handing purchases over). The Store Keeper role has both; the School Admin always has every page. See {{ page('roles-permissions', 'Roles, permissions and scope') }}.

Inventory: what the store sells

Inventory shows every item as a card: its picture, category, price, and how many are left against how many have been sold. The figures across the top count what is for sale, the units on the shelf, what is running low (5 or fewer left), what is sold out, what is waiting to be collected and this month's sales. The chips (Everything, In stock, Running low, Sold out, Hidden) and the search narrow the cards in place.

{{ shot('store', 'Inventory: each item with its picture, price and stock. Sell, Add stock and Hide are on its card.') }}
  • + Add an item opens a pop-up: the name, a category, the price, a description (sizes, colours, what is included), up to three pictures and how many are in stock. The first picture is the one on the item's card; a picture shows in its box as soon as you choose it, before anything is saved.
  • Click an item's name or picture to edit it in the same pop-up. Tick Remove under a picture to take it away.
  • Add stock on a card adds that many to the shelf. For a correction, enter a minus number (for example -2); stock never goes below nought.
  • Hide takes an item out of the parents' Store without deleting it or its sales; Show again (under Hidden) brings it back.
{{ shot('item-modal', 'Adding or editing an item: what it is, its price and up to three pictures, in a pop-up.') }}
An item can be bought again and again while any are left. When the last one goes it shows Sold out to parents and cannot be bought until you add stock.

Recording a payment taken at the office

For a store payment made in person — cash, a bank transfer or POS — use Record a payment (in the menu, or Sell on an item's card, which opens it in a pop-up with that item chosen). Choose the item and how many, find the student as you type, and say how it was paid; a transfer or POS needs its reference so the payment can be traced. The total follows as you go. Saving takes the items off the shelf at once and gives the purchase its receipt number (for example STR-000012); the parent sees it in their Store.

{{ shot('sell-modal', 'Record a payment: the item and how many, the student, how it was paid, and the running total.') }}

This is also how a school without online payments runs its store: parents can see the store and its prices in their portal, and pay at the office.

When parents pay online

If the school has its own online payments set up (Settings › Online payments, see {{ page('finance', 'Fees, payments and receipts') }}), a parent can buy from their portal: they choose how many for each of their children and pay on Paystack's secure page. As soon as Paystack confirms the payment, the stock goes down and the purchase appears under Purchase claims, exactly as if it had been recorded at the office. If two people buy the last one at the same moment, the second payment is still recorded (the money has come in) and marked Paid · out of stock: add stock and hand it over, or cancel it and refund the parent.

Purchase claims: handing over

Purchase claims lists everything bought, newest first, with what is To be collected shown first, fifty at a time. The chips switch between to be collected, collected, cancelled and everything; the search finds a student, an item, a receipt number or a payment reference.

{{ shot('claims', 'Purchase claims: what is waiting to be collected, with Hand over on each.') }}

Hand over opens the purchase in a pop-up. Say who collected it — the student, or a parent (choose from the parents linked to the child, or type a name) — and press Mark collected. The parent is sent a text saying what was collected and by whom, and the purchase moves to Collected with the date and who handed it over.

{{ shot('handover-modal', 'Handing a purchase over: who collected it, and Mark collected. The parent gets a text.') }}

A purchase not yet collected can be cancelled (recorded by mistake, or refunded): give a reason, and what it took goes back on the shelf. For an online payment, refund the parent through Paystack or by hand.

Who can do what

  • View the store: Inventory and Purchase claims, to look.
  • Run the store: add and edit items and their pictures, add stock, hide items, record payments, hand purchases over and cancel them.
  • Parents see their own Store, and only what was bought for their own children.
