The words this codebase and its users use, with where to read more.

TermMeaning
PlatformThe whole deployment: the registry, the console and every school. Also control_plane/.
School / tenantOne customer. Has a code, a database, a folder and addresses. "Tenant" is the code's word for it.
Code (slug)A school's permanent short identifier (alpha). Forms its address, database and folder. Never reused or renamed.
RegistryThe one database holding which schools exist, their addresses, the platform team, tickets, limits, the launch id.
Portal addressThe address the platform issues, <code>.<portal domain>. Permanent.
Platform hostAn address listed in BRIGHTSTARS_PLATFORM_HOSTS: serves the console, /marketing, /docs, never a school.
Super adminThe one overall platform admin who manages the team and reads every log, and can never be removed.
Platform adminAn operator with full control over every school but not the team or others' logs.
School AdminA school's top-level role (is_system = 1): everything in that school. Platform admins entering a school hold it.
Entry ticketA single-use, short-lived token that lets a platform admin into a school ({{ file('control_plane/entry.py') }}).
Reserved accountplatform@<username>: the account a platform admin uses inside a school.
Launch idA random value written at every server start; sessions carry it, which is how a restart signs people out.
WorkspaceEntrance Examination or School: the two areas staff choose between.
Permission / role / scopeAn ability; a bundle of abilities; a limit to particular classes, subjects or banks.
CandidateSomeone sitting the entrance examination. Not yet a student.
Entry groupyear7 (Year 7 / JSS 1) or year10 (Year 10 / SSS 1): which entrance papers apply.
BankA JSON file of questions in a school's data/ folder.
Configuration (entrance)The rule that makes a bank a live paper for an entry group, subject, session and term.
AttemptOne sitting of a test, examination or paper, with its frozen copy of the questions.
Frozen questionsThe copy of the questions taken when an attempt starts, so later edits cannot change it.
PracticeSelf-study: retakeable, shuffled, marked at once, never stored, never counted.
CAContinuous assessment: tests, assignments and projects, out of 40.
Exam / CA / term resultExam out of 60 plus CA out of 40, 100 in all, per subject and term.
Entered / verified / approved / releasedThe four steps a result passes before a student may see it.
Report cardA student's term summary, worked out on demand; ready when all official results of the term are released.
SessionAn academic year such as 2025/2026. (Not a browser session: the docs say "sign-in" or "cookie" for that.)
TermFirst, Second or Third Term; "Full Session" means the whole year.
Fee item / assessment / payment / allocationA catalogue line; a fee charged to a student; money received; the part of a payment applied to a fee.
VoidedA payment cancelled with a reason and kept in the record.
GuardianThe contact on a student's record who receives notices (email and phone). Distinct from a parent account, which is a sign-in linked to children.
DeliverySending email and SMS: a school's own accounts, with the platform's shared account as a fallback.
Best effortWork that must never block or undo the change that triggered it, such as notifications.
Numbering patternThe checked text a school uses to say how its codes look, such as {school}-{year}-{seq:4}.
Verification scriptAn end-to-end test in tests/verification/.
Contract testA fast test in tests/current/ guarding a rule of the design.
Archived studentA student who has left: active is 0 and archived_at is set. Their sign-in is refused, but their record, results and fees stay, and parents still see them.
Deactivated studentA student switched off without being archived. Hidden from current lists; the deactivate switch refuses an archived student.
Bulk importBringing records in from a spreadsheet, in three steps: students, enrolment history and past results.
OutcomeHow a student left a year: promoted, repeated, graduated or withdrawn. A graduated outcome archives the student.
Migrated resultA past result brought in by the results import: released at once, with a migration source and a workflow entry.
Bulk-import permissionschool.bulk_import: stands in for the permission each import step needs.
AudienceWho a release note is for: staff, family or student.
What's newThe notice of changes, shown to each person in their browser until they close it.
Setup checklistThe new-school steps (classes, subjects, students, fees), shown until the school closes it.
