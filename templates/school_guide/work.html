Five kinds of work sit under the School workspace's navigation rail — Assignments, Projects, Tests, Practice Tests and Examinations — and every one except Practice feeds the same term result once marked (see {{ page('results-report-cards', 'Results and report cards') }}).

Assignments

Assignments can be written work you mark by hand, or a quiz taken on-screen and marked automatically, timed or not. Open the list, choose New assignment, and set the class, subject, session and term it belongs to, which students it applies to, and — for a quiz — the questions themselves (a text, four options and the right one, exactly like a question bank question). Once students have started work, opening one assignment from the list shows every student's own answer or score, ready to mark or check.

Projects

Projects are practical, longer-running tasks — a model to build, a report to write — with their own due date. There is no on-screen quiz for a project: a student is simply marked as having completed it or not, with a score and a remark you add by hand once you have seen the work.

Tests and Examinations: two separate shelves for the same kind of work

Tests and Examinations work in exactly the same way — build them the same way you build an assignment's quiz, question by question — and both count fully toward a student's official term result, unlike practice below. The only difference between them is which list a piece of work sits on and which permission controls it, so a school can tell its own smaller, in-term tests apart from its bigger, formal end-of-term examinations, and decide separately who may create or edit each kind. Put a piece of work under whichever of the two your school calls it; nothing about how it behaves changes either way.

Practice tests

Practice is a self-study bank: nothing a student does there is ever written to the database — no attempt, no answers, no score — so it can never reach a term result or hold back a report card. Students can take and retake it as often as they like, and every run serves the questions in a fresh order.

Telling parents

New work and graded work notify the student and every linked parent in-app automatically; a guardian's email and WhatsApp are also used where the school has set those up (see Email & WhatsApp).
