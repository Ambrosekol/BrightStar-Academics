Running the school itself — its staff, its roles, its look, and who did what.

Staff accounts

Administration creates, edits and deactivates staff accounts, and assigns each one its roles and scope (see Roles, permissions and scope). A new account starts on a one-time password that must be changed at first sign-in.

Audit log

The Activity Log records who did what, to which record, from which address, and whether it succeeded — every release, every account change, every sign-in. Some actions also notify School Admins directly.

Controls and locks

Controls & Alerts lists things raised automatically that need a senior person's review, and lets a resource — a question bank during a live exam, say — be frozen against changes, with a reason, until it is unlocked.

How the portal looks

School Profile sets the school's name, motto, logo, brand colours and sign-in photographs — everything a receipt, a report card and every page of the portal wear as their own.

Email and WhatsApp

Email & WhatsApp is where the school connects its own mail server and WhatsApp Business account, so every automatic notice — a new report card, a payment, a released timetable — reaches a guardian on those channels too, not only in-app.
