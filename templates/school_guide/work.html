Four kinds of work sit under the School workspace, and they all feed the same term result once marked.

Assignments

Assignments can be written work you mark by hand, or a quiz taken on-screen and marked automatically, timed or not. Set the class, subject, session and term, choose which students it applies to, and — for a quiz — the questions.

Projects

Projects are practical, longer-running tasks with their own due date, scored and remarked individually once submitted or assessed.

Tests and examinations

Tests and examinations are formal, question-based assessments whose results become part of a student's official record — unlike practice, below.

Practice tests

Practice is a self-study bank: nothing a student does there is ever written to the database — no attempt, no answers, no score — so it can never reach a term result or hold back a report card. Students can take and retake it as often as they like, and every run serves the questions in a fresh order.

Telling parents

New work and graded work notify the student and every linked parent in-app automatically; a guardian's email and WhatsApp are also used where the school has set those up (see Email & WhatsApp).
