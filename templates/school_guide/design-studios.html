The design studios let the school design its own ID cards, report cards and receipts: the layout, the colours, the pictures and exactly which details appear, from a ready-made template or from a blank page. All three work the same way, so once you know one, you know them all.

Opening a studio

Design studios is in the menu under Management, Design & print. It shows a card for each studio you are allowed to open, with how things stand (for example, whether report cards already use the school's design).

The studio screen

A studio fills the whole browser window, like a design program, so every tool is on screen at once and the page never scrolls. Press the ⛶ button (top right) to hide the browser's own bars as well; press it again, or Esc, to bring them back. ← Exit (top left) leaves the studio.

{{ shot('studio-report', 'The report card studio with the results table chosen: the tools along the top, what to add on the left, the page in the middle, and the chosen item\'s settings on the right.') }}
  • Along the top: undo and redo, zoom (− and +, or Fit to see the whole page), Grid and Snap, the side or page you are working on, and on the right Templates, Lock and Save. The report card and receipt studios also have Preview PDF and the Use this design switch.
  • On the left, one tab at a time: Insert (the blocks, elements and shapes to add), Details (the person's or payment's details, one click each), Images (the school's own pictures) and Page or Card (its size, background colour, gradient and background picture).
  • In the middle, the page itself, drawn by the same code that prints it, with a made-up student, staff member or payment so you can see real-looking details.
  • On the right, three tabs: Properties (everything about the chosen item; it opens by itself when you choose something), Layers (every item, top first) and Versions (earlier saves). A settings section folds away under its heading; Position and size, Look and Box and lines start folded to save room.
  • The line under the page says what has happened: saved, changed but not saved yet, locked, or what went wrong.

Adding and arranging items

  • Add anything from the left: it appears in the middle of the page, chosen, ready to move.
  • Choose an item by clicking it. Shift-click adds more to the choice; drag a box round several on an empty part of the page to choose them all.
  • Move by dragging, or with the arrow keys. Items snap to the page's edges and centre and to each other (pink lines show what they line up with); turn Snap off for free movement, or turn on Grid to snap to half-millimetre steps.
  • Resize from the eight square handles; hold Shift on a corner to keep the shape. Turn it with the round handle above; hold Shift for 15° steps.
  • Line up several items with the alignment buttons (left, centre, right, top, middle, bottom), or space three or more evenly. With one item, they line it up with the page.
  • Layers sets what sits in front: To front, Forward, Back, To back. Hide keeps an item but leaves it off the printout; Lock stops it moving by accident.
  • Exact numbers: under Position and size, type the position, width, height and turn in millimetres and degrees.

Text and details

Text is words you type, the same on every copy ("Student Identity Card", "Official Receipt"). A detail is filled in for each person or payment: the student's name, the class, the amount paid. The Details tab lists every detail the studio knows; click one to put it on the page, already labelled ("Class: JSS 1"). A detail can be changed to any other from Shows in its settings, and its label edited or removed.

{{ shot('studio-idcard-details', 'The ID card studio\'s Details tab: every detail a student card can show, one click each.') }}

For text and details you can choose the font, size, weight, italics, underline, capitals, letter and line spacing, alignment (including justified) and up-and-down position, the colour, and a coloured box behind the words with rounded corners and padding. Words that do not fit their box are made smaller until they do, never cut off, down to a small minimum, so a long name still shows whole; make the box bigger if the type gets too small.

Pictures, signatures and the school's images

  • The photograph and the logo are each person's and the school's own. Choose how a picture fits its box (fill and crop, show it all, or stretch), clip it to a circle, rounded box, hexagon, shield, star or arch, give it a frame, or make it black and white.
  • Signatures: the head's (from Report card settings), the class teacher's own (from My signature), a staff member's own on a staff card, and the authorised signature on receipts (from Receipt settings). A missing signature shows as an empty box in the studio and is left blank when printed.
  • The school's images (the Images tab): upload a PNG or JPG (a watermark, a border, a badge, the school stamp) and click it to put it on the page, or choose it as the background under Page. The images are shared by all three studios. An image a locked design uses cannot be removed until that design is unlocked.

Colours, shapes and effects

Every colour can be the school's own colour or accent (so a design follows if the school's colours ever change), white, black, a recent colour, any colour you pick, or a typed code such as #1d6fd8. Shapes (rectangles, ellipses, triangles, diamonds, hexagons, stars, chevrons, arrows, waves, corner sweeps and lines) take a fill, a straight or round gradient, an outline of any thickness, solid, dashed or dotted, and rounded corners. Any item can be faded (opacity), given a soft or strong shadow, and turned.

Templates and versions

Templates shows complete ready-made designs (four for each studio), drawn in the school's own colours with its logo. Pick one to put it on the page; Undo brings back what was there before, and nothing is kept until you save.

{{ shot('studio-templates', 'Templates: four complete report card designs in the school\'s colours. Picking one replaces the page; Undo brings yours back.') }}

Every save is kept under Versions (the newest twenty), with who saved it and when. Bring back puts an earlier version on the page; save it to keep it.

Saving, locking and switching on

  • Save (or Ctrl+S) keeps the design. Until then, changes are only on your screen, and the page warns you before you leave it with changes not saved.
  • Lock saves it and stops anyone changing it by accident; Unlock allows changes again. Locking and unlocking are in the activity log.
  • ID cards print from the saved design straight away.
  • Report cards and receipts keep the standard design until you switch the school's on: press Use this design (the switch in the top bar). From then on every report card, or every receipt, is drawn from the saved design; press it again to go back to the standard one. Preview PDF shows the design on your screen (saved or not) as it will print, with a made-up student or payment, before you switch it on.

What each studio adds

The ID card studio

{{ shot('studio-idcard', 'The ID card studio: the student card with the photograph chosen, its picture settings on the right.') }}

A bank-card-sized card, front and back, landscape or portrait, with the QR code that opens the card's check page and a barcode of the card or admission number. Separate designs for student and staff cards (the switch at the top left). Printing, cancelling and replacing cards: see {{ page('students', 'Students') }}, ID cards.

The report card studio

An A4 page, portrait or landscape, and a second page printed only when something is on it. Its blocks are the results table (choose its columns), the summary, attendance, the trait ratings, the grading key and the class teacher's comment; each has its own fonts, sizes, heading band, stripes, lines and colours, and a table shares its space between its rows so eight subjects and sixteen both fit. Details include attendance, the position in class and the student's personal details. See {{ page('results-report-cards', 'Results and report cards') }}.

{{ shot('reportcard-designed', 'A parent\'s view of a report card in the school\'s own design (the Modern template).') }}

The receipt studio

{{ shot('studio-receipt', 'The receipt studio with the Paid stamp chosen: its word, shape and date on the right.') }}

An A5 or A4 page, either way round. Its blocks are the amount paid (in figures, and in words if wanted), the balance (fees for the session, paid so far, balance due) and a Paid stamp. Once switched on, the payment page, the printout, the PDF and the copy emailed to parents all follow it. See {{ page('finance', 'Fees, payments and receipts') }}.

Troubleshooting

I cannot see Design studios in the menu, or a studio is missing from it.
Each studio needs its own right (see the table above), and Design studios shows only the studios you may open. Ask the School Admin to add the right role to your account.
The studio says "Open this on a computer".
You are on a phone, or the browser window is narrower than 1024 pixels. Use a laptop or desktop; on a computer, make the window wider or zoom the browser out (Ctrl and −).
My changes have gone.
Changes are kept only when you press Save. If you saved and want an earlier design back, open Versions and press Bring back, then Save.
Save, Templates and the Add buttons are greyed out.
The design is locked. Press Unlock (top right) if you may change it; the status line says since when it has been locked.
I cannot move or change one item.
It is locked on its own: choose it in Layers and press Unlock. If you cannot click it at all, another item is in front of it: choose it in Layers instead, or move the one in front.
An item has disappeared.
It may be hidden (shown faded in Layers: press Show), behind another item (use To front), moved off the page (choose it in Layers and set its position under Position and size), or on the other side or page.
Text looks very small.
Text too long for its box is made smaller so it fits. Make the box wider or taller, shorten a label, or choose a smaller font size you are happy with.
The school's design is not used on report cards or receipts.
Press Use this design: until then the standard design is used. Report cards and receipts use the saved design, so save your latest changes. A change can take up to a minute to reach every copy.
A signature, the logo or a photograph is blank.
The picture has not been set: the head's signature on Report card settings, a teacher's own on My signature, the authorised signature on Receipt settings, the logo on the school profile, a photograph on the student's or staff member's page.
A detail is empty on a printout.
It is empty in the record itself (for example, no date of birth was entered). Fill it in on the student's or staff member's page, or the payment; the next copy shows it.
I cannot remove one of the school's images.
A locked design uses it. Unlock that design (the message says which), then remove the image.
The printout is the wrong size.
Print at 100% ("Actual size"), not "Fit to page". ID card sheets are A4; report cards are A4; receipts are the size chosen under the receipt studio's Page tab.
The PDF shows "N" instead of the ₦ sign.
The Helvetica, Times and Courier fonts cannot draw the Naira sign in a PDF, so N stands in for it. Choose the DejaVu Sans font for amounts to have ₦.
