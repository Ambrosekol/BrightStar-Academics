Library keeps track of every book the school owns, who has borrowed a copy, and when it is due back. It is a staff tool only: a student or parent has no library page of their own in the portal — if someone needs to know what is out or overdue, a member of staff with library access checks it for them here.

Adding and editing books

Add a book with its title, author, ISBN and however many copies the school owns. You can search the list by title or author at any time. Editing a book can add more copies (the school bought extra) or remove some (a copy was lost or damaged) — the count of copies currently out on loan is always kept separate from the count still on the shelf. A book cannot be archived while any of its copies is still out on loan; every copy must be returned first.

Issuing and returning

A copy can be issued to a student or to a member of staff, for however many days the school allows borrowing — this sets its due date automatically, so nobody has to calculate it by hand. Returning a copy makes it available to be issued to someone else again immediately. The list shows, at a glance, which loans are still within their time and which are already overdue.

Who can do this

Simply looking at the library — the books, who has what, what is overdue — needs View library. Adding a book, editing one, or issuing and returning a copy all need the separate Manage library permission, so a school can let more people see the library's state than can actually change it.
