{# Behaviour shared by the new/edit work forms (assignments, projects, tests, practice tests, examinations). A form marked data-guided has sections marked data-step-panel (each with data-title and data-hint). The script builds the step bar from them and shows one at a time: data-guided="steps" (new work) moves with Back and Next, and Next only goes on once that section's fields are filled in properly; data-guided="free" (editing) lets the person jump between the sections from the step bar and save from any of them. On submit every section is checked in order, and the first one with a mistake is opened. Without JavaScript every section simply shows, one after another.
